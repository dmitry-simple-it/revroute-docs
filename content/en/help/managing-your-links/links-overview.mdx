# RevRoute Links Overview

RevRoute Links is the modern link management platform for modern marketing teams.

With RevRoute, you can [create short links](/help/managing-your-links/links/how-to-create-link) using your own [custom domain](/help/managing-your-links/custom-domains/how-to-add-custom-domain) and get [world-class analytics](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/analytics-overview) on your links' performance.

## What can I do with RevRoute Links?

If you are a marketer or a business owner, RevRoute has the perfect suite of features for you to better manage your marketing campaigns with short links:

<Steps>

<Step title="Powerful link builder">

Our [link builder](/help/managing-your-links/additional-guides/link-builder) comes with features like:

- [UTM builder](/help/managing-your-links/links/utm-builder)
- Custom link previews
- [Device targeting](/help/managing-your-links/links/device-targeting)
- [Geo targeting](/help/managing-your-links/links/geo-targeting)
- [Password protection](/help/managing-your-links/links/password-protected-links)
- [Expiration dates](/help/managing-your-links/links/link-expiration)
- [Link cloaking](/help/managing-your-links/links/link-cloaking)

</Step>

<Step title="Use your own domain">

On RevRoute, you can create short links with your own [custom domain](/help/managing-your-links/custom-domains/how-to-add-custom-domain) – for better brand recognition.

We also have one of the most generous custom domain limits in the industry – you can add up to 3 custom domains to your workspace for free, and even more with our [paid plans](https://revroute.ru/pricing).

- [**Pro plan**](https://revroute.ru/pricing): 10 domains
- [**Business plan**](https://revroute.ru/pricing): 40 domains
- [**Enterprise plan**](https://revroute.ru/): Unlimited domains

</Step>

<Step title="Analytics that matter">

We provide you with [powerful analytics](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/analytics-overview) for your links, including geolocation, device, browser, and referrer information.

![RevRoute's Analytics dashboard](/images/ru/links/links-analytics.jpg)

Some of our analytics features include:

- [Timeseries analytics chart](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/analytics-overview#1-time-series-analytics-chart)
- [Real-time events stream](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/real-time-events-stream)
- [Exporting your analytics data](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/how-to-export-analytics)
- ["Ask AI" feature](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/analytics-overview#bonus-ask-ai-feature)

</Step>

<Step title="Free QR code generator">

QR codes and short links are like peas in a pod. That's why we've built a QR code generator right into RevRoute that lets you generate branded QR codes for your links.

</Step>

<Step title="Team collaboration">

RevRoute is built for teams. You can [invite your teammates](/help/managing-your-workspace/workspace-members/workspace-roles) to your account for free and manage your links together.

On RevRoute [Enterprise](https://revroute.ru/), you can also set up [SAML/SSO and SCIM Directory Sync](/help/managing-your-workspace/saml-sso/azure-scim) for greater security and control.

</Step>

<Step title="Enterprise-grade API">

If you're planning to use RevRoute to create links programmatically, we have a powerful, enterprise-grade API that allows you to do just that.

We also offer native SDKs for:

- TypeScript
- Python
- Go
- Ruby

</Step>

</Steps>

## How is RevRoute Links different from other alternatives?

We put a lot of care into the design and user experience of the platform, and we're constantly improving it.

One notable feature is the ability to customize the social media cards for your links, giving you full control over how your links look when shared on social media.

RevRoute also has a generous free plan. With RevRoute's free tier, you can:

- [Create links](/help/managing-your-links/links/how-to-create-link) with [custom domains](/help/managing-your-links/custom-domains/how-to-add-custom-domain) (add up to 3 domains)
- Update your link destination however many times you want
- Get [advanced analytics](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/analytics-overview) on your links's performance (including geolocation, device, browser, and referrer information)

Start using RevRoute today by [creating a workspace](/help/managing-your-workspace/what-is-a-workspace#how-to-create-a-workspace)!
