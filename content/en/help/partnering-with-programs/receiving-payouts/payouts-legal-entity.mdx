# Legal entity payouts: company details and settlement account

Programs pay companies to their settlement account through RevRoute's nominal account at T-Bank. No
contracts or receipts to sign per payout: you fill in the company and bank details once, and the bank
checks the company against the EGRUL registry.

By now you have applied to the program and created a partner account — if not, start with
[How to join a partner program](/help/partnering-with-programs/joining-programs/applying-to-a-program).

## Set your tax status [#set-your-tax-status]

### 1. Set status [#1-set-status]

Open **Payouts** in the left menu. Until the status is set, a warning **Set your tax status** stays at
the top of the page — click **Set status**. The same form opens from **Payout settings** at the top
right.

![Payouts page: 1 — the Set status button](/images/ru/partner/payouts/payouts-1-set-status.png)

### 2. Status: legal entity [#2-status-legal-entity]

In **Tax details** choose **Legal entity** — the form switches to company details.

### 3. Name, INN, OGRN, KPP and address [#3-company-details]

Company name — as in EGRUL. INN — 10 digits, OGRN — 13. KPP is optional: leave it empty if you don't
have one. Legal address — from the EGRUL extract: the bank checks the company against these details.

![Payout settings: 2 — status Legal entity, 3 — name, INN, OGRN, KPP and address](/images/ru/partner/payouts/payouts-llc-2-tax-form-top.png)

### 4. Registration date, phone and bank details [#4-registration-date-phone-and-bank-details]

Registration date — from EGRUL. Phone is optional but useful if the bank has questions about the
company. In **Bank details**: BIC and bank name, settlement and correspondent account — the money
arrives to this account.

### 5. Save [#5-save]

Click **Save**. The badge changes to **Profile complete** and the warning on the payouts page changes
to "Payout setup not finished" until the bank's check completes.

![Bottom of the form: 4 — registration date, phone and bank details, 5 — the Save button](/images/ru/partner/payouts/payouts-llc-3-tax-form-bottom.png)

## Bank check [#bank-check]

After saving, RevRoute passes the details to the bank for every program that pays legal entities. In
the **Settlement account payouts** block each program gets a status:

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Checking** | Nothing: the bank is verifying the company against EGRUL. |
| **Rejected by the bank** | The bank didn't confirm the company in EGRUL — compare INN, OGRN and name with the extract and save again. |
| **Setup not finished** | Nothing: the program is still setting up payouts to legal entities. |
| **Ready** | Everything is set up, the payout goes through automatically. |

The details are shared across programs: pass the check once and you get **Ready** everywhere a program
pays legal entities.

## Money and documents [#money-and-documents]

The program starts the payout once commissions pass the holding period and reach the minimum amount.
Nothing to sign: after the program confirms the payout, the money goes to the company's settlement
account; the amount sent to the bank is in whole rubles.

Each payout's status is on the [Payouts](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts) page; why a payout may be
delayed — in the [FAQs](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts#faqs).
