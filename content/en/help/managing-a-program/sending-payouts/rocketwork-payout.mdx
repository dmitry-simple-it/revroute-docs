# How to pay a partner through RocketWork

Programs pay self-employed partners and sole proprietors through RocketWork: money leaves your
company's RocketWork balance, a one-off contract is issued for each payout, and the self-employed
partner's NPD receipt is generated automatically. You need an earned commission, enough balance and a
payout confirmation.

By now the partner has set the tax status and connected RocketWork — their status is **Ready** (see
the [self-employed](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/payouts-self-employed) and
[sole proprietor](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/payouts-sole-proprietor) guides).

## Create a commission [#create-a-commission]

Commissions usually appear on their own from sales through partner links. A one-off payout (bonus,
reimbursement) can be added manually.

### 1. Create commission [#1-create-commission]

Open **Commissions** in the program menu and click **Create commission** (or press `C`).

![Commissions: 1 — the Create commission button](/images/ru/program/rocketwork-payout/rw-payout-1-commissions.png)

### 2. Partner [#2-partner]

Pick the partner from the list — start typing the name.

### 3. One-time type [#3-one-off]

**One-time** — a bonus or reimbursement not tied to a sale. It is payable immediately: the holding
period doesn't apply.

### 4. Amount [#4-amount]

Enter the amount in rubles. The description is optional and visible to the partner.

### 5. Save [#5-save]

Click **Create commission**. It appears in the list, and a pending payout to the partner appears in
**Payouts**.

![Create commission: 2 — partner, 3 — One-time type, 4 — amount, 5 — the Create commission button](/images/ru/program/rocketwork-payout/rw-payout-2-create.png)

## Check the RocketWork balance [#check-the-rocketwork-balance]

### 6. Balance and top-up details [#6-balance-and-top-up]

Open **Payouts**. At the top is the **RocketWork account** card with the current balance. If it
doesn't cover pending payouts, click **Top-up details** and transfer money from the company account.
Mind RocketWork's fee: it is charged from the same balance at your rate.

### 7. Confirm payouts [#7-confirm-payouts]

The **Pending payouts** card shows the amount ready to pay. Click **Confirm payouts**.

![Payouts: 6 — RocketWork balance and top-up details, 7 — the Confirm payouts button](/images/ru/program/rocketwork-payout/rw-payout-3-balance.png)

## Confirm the payout [#confirm-the-payout]

### 8. Check the totals [#8-check-the-totals]

The **Confirm payouts** window shows where the money comes from and what each partner gets:

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | Commissions earned by partners for the period |
| **RevRoute fee** | 5 % of payouts made through RevRoute |
| **Partners receive** | What partners receive after the fee |
| **RocketWork funding budget** | What is charged to the RocketWork balance |
| **Legal entities receive** | Payouts to companies via the nominal account, if any |

### 9. Press and hold [#9-press-and-hold]

Press and hold the button at the bottom. While the balance is insufficient the button is disabled —
top up (step 6) and come back.

![Confirm payouts: 8 — totals, 9 — the confirm button](/images/ru/program/rocketwork-payout/rw-payout-4-confirm.png)

## What happens next [#whats-next]

After confirmation RevRoute creates a one-off contract in RocketWork for each payout. The partner gets
an email and signs the contract with an SMS code; after signing RocketWork transfers the money and
issues the closing documents. Each payout's status is shown in **Payouts**.

General rules — minimum amount, holding period, payout statuses — are in
[Sending partner payouts](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts).
