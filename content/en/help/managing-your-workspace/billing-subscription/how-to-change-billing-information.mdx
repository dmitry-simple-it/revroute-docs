First of all, thank you for upgrading to a paid plan on Revroute!

Your support helps us keep building and improving Revroute. This guide walks you through updating the card we charge for your subscription.

## How billing works on Revroute

Revroute uses [YooKassa](https://yookassa.ru) to process subscription payments. The first time you subscribe, you save a card through the YooKassa payment widget — the card is tokenized and linked to your workspace as a `payment_method_id`. All subsequent renewals charge that token automatically.

This means:

- Your card details are not stored by Revroute — only the YooKassa token (a `synonym`).
- You can replace the saved card at any time from your billing settings.
- The card on file is shown in a masked format (`Visa •••• 4242`).

## Replacing your saved card

<Steps>

<Step title="Open billing settings">

From your workspace dashboard (e.g. `app.revroute.ru/<your-workspace>`), click **Settings → Billing**.

You'll see the active subscription, the next renewal date, and the card currently on file.

</Step>

<Step title="Click Replace card">

Under **Payment method**, click **Replace card**.

This opens the YooKassa payment widget. You'll be asked to enter your new card details. YooKassa performs a small zero-amount authorization (1 ₽ or similar) to verify the card, then refunds it immediately.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm">

Once the new card is verified, the previous token is discarded and all future renewals charge the new card.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your card expires or your bank reissues it with a new PAN, please replace
  it before the next renewal date — otherwise the renewal will fail and your
  workspace may be downgraded to the free plan.
</Tip>

## Updating your billing email and company details

The card is one thing; the legal entity that owns the subscription is another. To update the **company name, INN, KPP, or billing email** that appears on your closing documents:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoice details**.
2. Update your company name, INN, KPP and billing email.
3. Save — your next invoice will reflect the new details.

<Note>
  Changes to billing details are applied to **future** invoices only. For
  corrections to invoices that have already been issued, reach out to
  [support@revroute.ru](mailto:support@revroute.ru).
</Note>

## Downloading invoices

Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoices** for a list of all subscription receipts. Each invoice includes the YooKassa transaction ID for your accounting team.

## Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel any time from **Settings → Billing → Manage subscription**. You'll keep access to paid features until the end of the current billing period; no further charges will be made to your card after cancellation.
