# What is a "Workspace" on RevRoute?

On RevRoute, a "Workspace" is a space that holds the short links, domains, analytics, and partner program for your website, app, or business.

You can think of a RevRoute workspace as a "Team" or an "Organization" where you can [invite your teammates](/help/managing-your-workspace/workspace-members/workspace-roles) to work together.

To switch between workspaces or create a new one, open the workspace menu by clicking the workspace logo in the top-left corner. The menu also has **Settings** and **Invite members** buttons.

![Workspace menu: the current workspace, the list of workspaces, and the Create workspace button](/images/ru/workspace/workspace-switcher.jpg)

## How to create a workspace

Open the workspace menu and click **Create workspace**.

![Create a workspace dialog: name, slug, and logo](/images/ru/workspace/workspace-create.jpg)

Here are the fields in the workspace creation dialog:

1. **Workspace name**: the name of your company or product.
2. **Workspace slug**: your workspace's unique identifier in the `app.revroute.ru/<slug>` address. It's also used in your partner program's address.
3. **Workspace logo**: optional; the recommended size is 160×160 px.

Once you have filled in the fields, click **Create workspace**.

## Onboarding checklist

In the bottom-right corner of your workspace, the **Complete setup** button opens a checklist of first steps:

![Complete setup checklist: connect a domain, create a program, set up conversion tracking, and invite partners](/images/ru/workspace/onboarding-checklist.jpg)

1. **Connect a domain** — a [custom domain](/help/managing-your-links/custom-domains/how-to-add-custom-domain) for your short links.
2. **Create a program** — your [partner program](/help/managing-a-program/getting-started/setting-up-your-program).
3. **Set up conversion tracking** — to see leads and sales, not just clicks ([developer guide](/docs/quickstart/server)).
4. **Invite partners** — [invitations](/help/managing-a-program/recruiting-partners/inviting-partners) to your program.

Completed steps are checked off. The **Hide tips** button hides the checklist.

## How many domains can I add to a workspace?

[Custom domains](/help/managing-your-links/custom-domains/how-to-add-custom-domain) are available on every plan, including the free RevRoute Links Free plan. The number of domains you can connect depends on your plan:

- **RevRoute Links**: Free — 1 domain, Pro — 10, Business — 50;
- **RevRoute Partners**: Business — 100 domains, Advanced — 250;
- **Enterprise** plans of both product lines — by custom agreement.

See the [pricing page](/pricing) for current terms.

If your workspace has multiple domains, you can filter links by domain: **Filter** → **Domain**.

![Filtering links by domain: the list of domains with link counts](/images/ru/workspace/links-filter-domain.jpg)

## Billing for workspaces

On RevRoute, you can create several workspaces, and each one is billed separately. A workspace's plan and billing cycle are shown in **Settings** → **Billing**. To learn how to change your plan or cancel your subscription, see [this article](/help/managing-your-workspace/billing-subscription/how-to-cancel-subscription).
