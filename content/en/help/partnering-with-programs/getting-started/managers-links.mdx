# Several managers in one partner account

Managers don't need to sign up separately or join the program again. You keep one partner account, invite your managers to it and create a link for each of them. Clicks, leads and sales are counted per link, so you can see what each manager brought in.

> Screenshots show the Russian interface; button names are given in both languages.

## Invite your managers

### 1. Profile icon

In the left panel of the partner dashboard, click the profile icon.

### 2. Members

In the profile menu, open **Members** («Участники»).

### 3. Invite member

Click **Invite member** («Пригласить участника») in the top right corner, or press `M`.

![Members page: 1 — profile icon, 2 — Members item, 3 — Invite member button](/images/ru/partner/managers/managers-1-members.png)

### 4. Email and role

Enter the manager's email and keep the **Member** («Участник») role. **Add email** adds another row, so you can invite several people at once.

### 5. Send the invite

Click **Send invite** («Отправить приглашение»). The manager receives an email with a link; the invite is valid for 14 days.

![Invite dialog: 4 — email and role, 5 — Send invite button](/images/ru/partner/managers/managers-2-invite.png)

> The **Member** role works with programs and links but can't manage the team or payout details — that stays with the owner. More on roles in [Inviting your partner team](/help/partnering-with-programs/getting-started/managing-partner-teams).

## Create a link for each manager

### 6. Open the program's links

Go to the program and open **Links** («Ссылки») in the left menu.

### 7. Create link

Click **Create link** («Создать ссылку»), or press `C`.

![Program Links page: 6 — Links menu item, 7 — Create link button](/images/ru/partner/managers/managers-3-links.png)

### 8. Short link with the manager's name

In **Short link** («Короткая ссылка»), enter a clear name such as `your-company-anna`. You can leave **Destination URL** empty — the link will lead to the program's website.

### 9. Comment

In **Comments** («Комментарии»), note whose link it is: "Anna Smith, manager". Comments are visible to everyone in the account.

### 10. Create the link

Click **Create link** and hand it to the manager. Repeat steps 7–10 for each manager.

![New link dialog: 8 — short link, 9 — comment, 10 — Create link button](/images/ru/partner/managers/managers-4-new-link.png)

> If **Create link** is disabled, your group has reached the program's link limit or the program doesn't let partners create their own links. Contact the program's support.

## See results per manager

### 11. Clicks, leads and sales per link

The **Links** page shows clicks, leads and sales for every link. The icon next to the period switches the view: the compact list shows all-time totals, cards show charts for the selected period. Click a link to open analytics for that link only.

![Managers' links: 11 — clicks, leads and sales per link](/images/ru/partner/managers/managers-5-stats.png)

## Good to know

- **Rewards go to the partner account as a whole**, not to a specific manager. Links show who brought what; splitting the rewards inside your company is up to you.
- **Everyone in the account sees all links.** A link is tied to a manager only by its name and comment.
- **If a manager needs separate commissions and payouts**, they need their own partner account with their own program enrollment.
