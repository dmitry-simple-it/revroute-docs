# Reviewing program applications

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Business plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

## Where to find your program applications

In the program menu, open **Applications** under **Partners**. The count badge next to it shows how many applications are waiting for review.

![Applications page: applicant list and the count badge in the program menu](/images/ru/program/applications/applications-list.jpg)

## Applicant table

On your applications table, you'll be able to view key information, including:

- The applicant's name
- When they applied
- The group they applied to
- Their country
- Their website and social media profiles — e.g. YouTube and LinkedIn

**Filter** narrows applications down by group and country, and the search box finds an applicant by name, email, or company. Click on any of the rows to open the application.

## Applications menu

The **⋮** menu in the top right corner of the page has:

- **Auto-approval settings** — opens the default group's settings (see [Auto-approve](#auto-approve));
- **View rejected partners** — the list of rejected applications (see [below](#approving-a-previously-rejected-partner));
- **Export as CSV** — downloads the applications.

![Applications page menu](/images/ru/program/applications/applications-menu.jpg)

## Partner application

The application sheet brings together everything the applicant shared:

- the **About partner** tab — answers to your [application form](/help/managing-a-program/recruiting-partners/program-application-form) questions, description, website, and social profiles;
- on the right — name, email, country, and application date;
- **Risk analysis** — see [risk flags](#applicant-risk-flags);
- **Program activity** — how many RevRoute programs the partner is active in and how many they are banned from;
- **Group** — the group they applied to; change it with **Change**.

On the **Comments** tab, your team can leave notes about the applicant — they're visible only in your workspace. The **Message** button opens a conversation with the applicant in **Messages**, and the arrows at the top switch between applications.

![Application sheet: form answers, risk analysis, and the Reject and Approve buttons](/images/ru/program/applications/application-details.jpg)

### Online presence

Partners add their website and social accounts to their profile and can verify that they own them. Verification is optional but speeds up review: if no platforms are added or verified, a matching risk flag appears in the risk analysis.

## Applicant risk flags

<Warning>
  We recommend reviewing flagged applicants and the information they've
  provided before making a final decision.
</Warning>

The **Risk analysis** block in the application sheet shows the overall risk level — **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** — and the flags behind it. The **Open** button shows all flags with explanations.

![Risk analysis dialog: Medium level with two flags](/images/ru/program/applications/application-risk.jpg)

These signals are powered by [fraud detection](/help/managing-a-program/insights-and-reporting/fraud-detection):

| Flag                                | Level  | Description                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fraud report                        | High   | This partner has been flagged for suspected fraud by another program.                                                    |
| Cross-program ban                   | High   | This partner has been banned from one or more other RevRoute programs, indicating a potentially high-risk history.       |
| Duplicate payout method             | High   | This partner is using a payout method already linked to another partner account, which may indicate duplicate accounts or fraud. |
| No website or social links added    | Medium | This partner hasn't provided any website or social links, making verification difficult.                                |
| Email domain mismatch with website  | Low    | The custom email domain doesn't match the provided website.                                                              |
| Masked email address                | Low    | Uses an anonymized email address. Not harmful, but harder to verify and to contact directly.                              |
| No verified website or social links | Low    | This partner hasn't verified their website or socials, making verification difficult.                                   |

## Approving applicants

There are 3 ways to approve pending applicants into your program.

### From the application sheet

Click **Approve** at the bottom of the application sheet (or press `A`) — the applicant joins your program right away.

### Bulk approve

Select applicants with the checkboxes on the left (the header checkbox selects everyone) and click **Approve** above the table.

![A selected application with the Approve and Reject buttons above the table](/images/ru/program/applications/applications-bulk.jpg)

### Auto-approve

Auto-approve is set per [partner group](/help/managing-a-program/getting-started/partner-groups): open the group, go to the **Settings** tab, and turn on **Enable auto-approve** under **Additional settings**. For the default group, **Auto-approval settings** in the applications menu leads there too.

![Enable auto-approve toggle in the group settings](/images/ru/program/groups/group-auto-approve.jpg)

RevRoute asks you to confirm. Check **Apply to all groups** to enable auto-approve in every group of the program at once.

![Confirm enabling auto-approve dialog](/images/ru/program/groups/group-auto-approve-confirm.jpg)

From then on, new applications to the group are approved automatically without review. Existing pending applications still need manual approval. Turning auto-approve off works the same way — with the toggle and a confirmation.

## Rejecting applicants

You can reject an application from the [application sheet](#partner-application) — with **Reject** or the `R` key — or several at once by [selecting them in the table](#bulk-approve).

A **Reject application** confirmation dialog opens first. If you suspect fraud, check **Select this if you believe the application shows signs of fraud**: the report helps protect other RevRoute programs — the partner gets a "Fraud report" flag. **Reject** rejects the application, **Cancel** backs out.

![Reject application confirmation dialog](/images/ru/program/applications/application-reject.jpg)

A rejected applicant can't join your program unless you approve their application yourself.

### Approving a previously rejected partner

If you rejected a partner by mistake or changed your mind, open the **⋮** menu on the applications page and select **View rejected partners**. In the **Rejected applications** list, open the application and click **Approve** — the partner joins your program right away.

![Rejected applications list (empty in the demo program)](/images/ru/program/applications/applications-rejected.jpg)
