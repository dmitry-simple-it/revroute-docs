# Setting business details in partner payout invoices

Every time you [receive a payout](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts) on RevRoute, our platform automatically generates an invoice for accounting purposes.

With RevRoute, you can also set your business details (company name, address, tax ID) in your payout invoices for tax compliance.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the payout settings modal">

First, go to the [Payouts page](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts) and click the **Payout settings** button:

![Payouts page: summary, payouts table and the Payout settings button](/images/ru/partner/payouts.jpg)

</Step>
<Step title="Enter your business details">
This will open up the Payout settings modal, where you can set your business name, address, and tax ID:

![Invoice details block: organization name, legal address and tax ID](/images/ru/partner/invoice-details.jpg)

</Step>

<Step title="Save changes and view updated invoice">

Once the changes are saved, they are automatically reflected in your payout invoices:


</Step>
</Steps>
