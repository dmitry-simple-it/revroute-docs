As an affiliate partner on Revroute, you can participate in various affiliate programs and receive commission payouts directly to your bank card or via SBP (Faster Payments System).

Once you've set up a [partner profile on Revroute](https://partners.revroute.ru/), you can connect your payout method to start receiving payouts from the programs you're working with.

## Supported payout methods

Revroute pays partners via [YooKassa Payouts](https://yookassa.ru/developers/payouts). You can choose between:

| Method            | Description                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bank card**     | Russian-issued bank card (Mir, Visa/Mastercard from a Russian bank). Saved as a tokenized synonym via the YooKassa widget — Revroute never sees your card number. |
| **SBP**           | Transfer by phone number to one of the supported banks (full list under "Add SBP destination"). |

<Note>
  Payouts are sent in **RUB only**. International cards or non-RU bank accounts
  are not supported.
</Note>

## Connecting a bank card

<Steps>

<Step title="Open payout settings">

Go to your [payout settings on Revroute](https://partners.revroute.ru/settings/payouts) and click **Add card**.

</Step>

<Step title="Save the card via the YooKassa widget">

A YooKassa widget will open and ask you to enter your card details. The card will be tokenized — the token is stored against your partner profile and used for future payouts. Your card number is never visible to Revroute or to the program owner.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm with a test withdrawal (optional)">

For some banks YooKassa performs a small no-cost verification. Once accepted, your card appears in **Payout methods** with the masked PAN (`••2345`).

</Step>

</Steps>

## Connecting an SBP destination

<Steps>

<Step title="Open payout settings">

Go to your [payout settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/settings/payouts) and click **Add SBP**.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your phone number and pick a bank">

Choose the bank that should receive your SBP payouts. The list comes from YooKassa and includes the major Russian banks: Sberbank, T-Bank, Alfa-Bank, VTB, Raiffeisenbank, Gazprombank, Sovcombank, and more.

</Step>

<Step title="Save">

Your SBP destination appears in **Payout methods**. You can switch between SBP and a saved card any time from the same screen.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Setting up tax status

Before your first payout, set your **tax status** in your partner profile:

- **Self-employed (NPD regime)** — you generate a receipt in the Moy Nalog ("My Tax") app for each payout. Tax: 4% on payments from individuals, 6% on payments from businesses.
- **Sole proprietor (IP)** — you handle taxes according to your own tax regime.
- **Private individual** — the program is your tax agent and withholds personal income tax (NDFL) from each payout before sending it to you.

<Warning>
  Revroute does not file or remit taxes on your behalf. Choose the status that
  matches your legal situation in Russia. If the program acts as your tax
  agent, your payout amount will be displayed before tax — the actual amount
  arriving on your card or SBP destination will be 13% lower (NDFL for residents).
</Warning>

## FAQs

### Why doesn't Revroute see my full card number?

Cards are tokenized inside the YooKassa widget. The widget posts directly to YooKassa, which returns a token (a `synonym`) that Revroute stores. We never receive the raw card number, CVV, or expiry — this keeps Revroute outside of PCI DSS scope.

### Can I receive payouts in USD or to an international card?

No. Payouts run through YooKassa Payouts, which sends RUB only and only to Russian cards / SBP. If your bank is outside Russia, payouts are not supported at this time.

### Why is my payout still pending?

Your payout is still pending because the program hasn't paid its affiliates yet, or because you haven't reached the program's minimum payout amount (if one is configured). Check the program's payout schedule in their announcements or reach out to them directly.

### My SBP transfer didn't arrive — what should I do?

SBP transfers are usually instant, but some banks queue them at night or weekends. If more than 24 hours have passed, contact your bank with the transfer details from your payout email (date, amount, sender — `ЮMoney`). If your bank cannot find the payment, reach out to Revroute support.

### Can I switch from card to SBP, or vice versa?

Yes — go to your [payout settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/settings/payouts) and add the new destination. The most recently configured method becomes your default. You can also delete the old one if you don't need it.

### Do I need to manually withdraw my earnings from Revroute?

No. As soon as the program confirms a payout to you, Revroute dispatches it through YooKassa to your configured card / SBP destination. You don't need to log in to claim anything.
