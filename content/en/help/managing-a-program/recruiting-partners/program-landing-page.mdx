# Creating a landing page for your partner program

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Business plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

On RevRoute, you can create a branded landing page with details about your [partner program](/help/managing-a-program/partners-overview).

## What is the program landing page?

The program landing page tells potential partners about your program and the rewards offered. You can add a product description, brand materials, FAQ sections, and any other content that helps an applicant understand why they should apply.

The landing page is published at `partners.revroute.ru/<program-slug>` — for example, [partners.revroute.ru/cloudflow-demo](https://partners.revroute.ru/cloudflow-demo) for the CloudFlow demo program. Its **Apply** button leads to your [application form](/help/managing-a-program/recruiting-partners/program-application-form).

<Tip>
  On RevRoute, you can create a separate landing page for each [partner
  group](/help/managing-a-program/getting-started/partner-groups) — e.g. different
  pages for bloggers, integrators, and resellers. A group's landing page lives at
  `partners.revroute.ru/<program-slug>/<group-slug>`.

A new partner group starts with a copy of the default group's landing page,
which you can then edit separately.

</Tip>

## Where to find the landing page builder?

On the **Groups** page, open the group you want to customize the landing page for:

![Partner groups list](/images/ru/program/groups/groups-list.jpg)

Open the **Branding** tab and choose **Landing page** in the switcher above the editor. The **Branding** item in the program menu (under **Configuration**) also leads to the default group's landing page.

![Branding tab: brand elements on the left, landing page preview on the right](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-editor.jpg)

On the left are the **Brand elements**: logo (required), wordmark, and brand color, saved with their own **Save** button. On the right is the landing page preview, which you edit in place. The **Open** button above the preview opens the landing page in a new tab.

## Editing and adding content

Hover over a landing page block to reveal its actions: **Edit** (pencil), arrows to move the block up or down, and **Delete** (trash). The **Insert block** button between blocks adds a new block at that spot.

![Two content blocks with edit, move, and delete actions on hover](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-block-actions.jpg)

The top section is the same on every landing page: eyebrow, heading, description, the **Rewards** block, and the **Apply** button. The heading and description can only be edited — this keeps every group's page consistent. Rewards come from the [group settings](/help/managing-a-program/rewards-and-commissions/partner-rewards). Blocks below the **Apply** button can be added, edited, removed, and reordered.

## Content types

Clicking **Insert block** lets you pick the content type:

![Insert block: text, image, files, accordion, and earnings calculator](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-insert-block.jpg)

### Text

Best for describing your product and program terms. Set a **Section title** and **Content** — the content supports Markdown (see the [table below](#markdown-styles-available)).

![Add text dialog](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-block-text.jpg)

### Image

For product screenshots and other visuals. Upload an SVG, JPG, PNG, or WebP file up to 5 MB and fill in the **Alt text**.

![Add image dialog](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-block-image.jpg)

### Files

A list of downloadable files — e.g. logos or a product deck. For each file, set a **Display name**, **File URL**, and an optional **Description**; **Add item** adds another file. Files aren't uploaded to RevRoute — the block links to them by URL.

![Add files dialog](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-block-files.jpg)

### Accordion

Collapsible items — best for FAQ sections. Each item has a **Title** and **Content**; **Add item** adds another one, and you can reorder items by dragging the handle on the left.

![Add accordion dialog with two questions](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-block-accordion.jpg)

### Earnings calculator

Gives partners a sense of how much they can earn. Set the **Average product price** (in rubles) and the **Billing period**: monthly, yearly, or one-time. Earnings are calculated from your program's default reward, and visitors pick the number of sales with a slider.

![Add earnings calculator dialog with a preview](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-block-calculator.jpg)

## Generating your landing page with AI

The AI landing page generator can quickly scaffold a page from your website: it reads the site's content and drafts blocks about your company, pricing, features, and more.

Click **Generate** above the preview and check the **Website URL** — it's prefilled from your program settings. Then click **Generate** in the dialog.

![Generate a new landing page dialog](/images/ru/program/branding/landing-generate.jpg)

<Warning>
  AI can make mistakes – make sure to review all generated content for accuracy
  and style before publishing your changes.
</Warning>

## Publishing your changes

Until you publish, the editor shows an **Unsaved draft** label and partners keep seeing the previous version of the page. The draft is stored only in your browser, so teammates see your changes once they're published.

When you're done, click **Publish** in the top right corner of the editor. The **⋮** menu next to it has an **Unpublish** option.

## Markdown styles available

| Content         | Example                                 |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Headings        | `# H1`, `## H2`, `### H3`               |
| Bold            | `**bold text**`                         |
| Italic          | `*italicized text*`                     |
| Blockquote      | `> blockquote`                          |
| Horizontal rule | `---`                                   |
| Link            | `[title](https://www.example.com)`      |
| Ordered list    | `1. First item`, `2. Second item`       |
| Unordered list  | `- First item`, `- Second item`         |
