# How to filter analytics by tags

On RevRoute, you can filter analytics by [tags](/help/managing-your-links/links/how-to-use-tags). This allows you to create powerful campaign-specific reports for your links.

For example, you're running a promo campaign with Telegram and VK bloggers with multiple links and you want to see how they're performing.

You can group the links with a shared tag — in the example below, **акции** ("promos") — and then filter the analytics by that tag to get a comprehensive report of how they're performing.

---

Before you can filter analytics by tags, you need to [create a tag](/help/managing-your-links/links/how-to-use-tags#how-to-create-a-tag) and [assign it to the links you want to filter by](/help/managing-your-links/links/how-to-use-tags#can-i-assign-multiple-tags-to-a-link).

<CheckList>
  <CheckListItem
    title="Create a tag"
    href="/help/managing-your-links/links/how-to-use-tags#how-to-create-a-tag"
  >
    If you haven't already, create a tag to filter your analytics by.
  </CheckListItem>
  <CheckListItem
    title="Assign the tag to the relevant links"
    href="/help/managing-your-links/links/how-to-use-tags#can-i-assign-multiple-tags-to-a-link"
  >
    Add the tag to all the links you want to filter by.
  </CheckListItem>
</CheckList>

Then, as your links start accruing clicks, you will be able to filter your analytics by tags.

<Steps>

    <Step title="Open the filter menu">
        In the [Analytics tab](https://app.revroute.ru/analytics) of your RevRoute workspace (the **Analytics** item under the **Statistics** section of the sidebar), click on the **Filter** button to open the filter menu:

        ![The Filter menu opened on the analytics page: Folder, Tag, Domain, Link and other filters](/images/ru/links/analytics-filter-dropdown.jpg)

        <Tip>
          You can also use the `F` keyboard shortcut to quickly open the filter menu.
        </Tip>
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select the 'Tag' filter and pick a tag">
        Select the **Tag** option in the menu and check the tag you want to filter by. A filter chip appears above the chart, and the click, lead, and sale metrics recalculate for the selected tag:

        ![The Tag submenu in the analytics filter: the "акции" tag is selected and the metrics are recalculated](/images/ru/links/analytics-filter-tag.jpg)

        <Info>
          The filter list only shows tags that have recorded events in the selected time period. If you don't see a tag, widen the date range in the date picker.
        </Info>
    </Step>

    <Step title="Optional: Filter by multiple tags">
        You can also filter by multiple tags by selecting multiple tags in the same menu.
    </Step>

</Steps>

Voila! You have successfully filtered your analytics by tags. You can also [export your analytics data](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/how-to-export-analytics) to a CSV file for further analysis.
