# Customer insights

One of the most important parts of marketing is accurate attribution – understanding how your marketing efforts and spend are converting to customer acquisition and revenue.

With RevRoute, you can turn your short links into end-to-end attribution engines by tracking how your clicks convert to [signups and sales](/docs/concepts/attribution).

As part of lead tracking, RevRoute creates customer objects to visualize how your users interact with your links and how much they spend on your product.

The **Customers** section gives you a deeper understanding of your customers' demographics, purchasing behavior, and lifetime value (LTV).

## Customers overview

The **Customers** section in the sidebar shows a list of all the customers in your workspace.

![Customers table on RevRoute](/images/ru/links/customers-table.jpg)

You can search for a specific customer by their email or name, and the ⚙ icon above the table lets you configure which columns are shown.

### Available columns

The data columns available to you are:
| Column | Description |
| -- | -- |
| Customer | The customer's name and email |
| Country | The customer's country |
| LTV | The total amount of revenue the customer has generated over time |
| Created | The date the customer was created |
| Paid | The date the customer made their first purchase |
| Cancelled | The date the customer canceled their subscription |

## Individual customer pages

For a given customer, you can also open their individual customer page to see their sales history.

![Customer page on RevRoute](/images/ru/program/customer-view.jpg)

This is useful for understanding which of your links a given customer interacted with before signing up for your app, allowing you to optimize your campaigns accordingly.

At the top of the page, you can see the customer's first sale date, time to sale, lifetime value, and, if their subscription was cancelled, the cancellation date.

The right-hand column shows the customer's profile and the attribution details available for them.

## Partner program customers

If the customer was referred by a partner, their page gets a **Partner earnings** tab showing which partner referred them, along with the sale amounts and the commissions earned on them.

Learn more about [RevRoute partner programs](/help/managing-a-program/partners-overview).
