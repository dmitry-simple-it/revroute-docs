# Frequently Asked Questions

We've compiled the most common partner questions in one place, so you can quickly find answers and get back to growing your earnings.

## Account & Settings

### How can I change my country?

You can change your country under your [partner profile settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/profile).

For compliance reasons, you cannot change your country once you've [connected payouts on RevRoute](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts). If you still need to change your country, please [reach out to support](https://revroute.ru/contact/support).

### How can I change my account type – Business / Individual?

You can change your account type under your [partner profile settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/profile).

For compliance reasons, you cannot change your account type once you've [connected payouts on RevRoute](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts). If you still need to change it, please [reach out to support](https://revroute.ru/contact/support).

### How can I update my affiliate link?

You can update your affiliate link under the Links tab of your program.

### How can I update my name, email, or phone number?

You can update your personal details under the **Profile** tab in your [partner profile](https://partners.revroute.ru/profile). Payout details — your name, tax ID and phone, or your company details — are set separately, in the **Tax details** block of your [payout settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts).

## Payouts & Earnings

### Why is my payout still pending?

Your payout is still [pending](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/commissions-payouts#payout-statuses) because the program hasn't confirmed it yet. Please reach out directly to your program to learn more about their payout schedule.

Some programs also require a minimum payout amount before your payouts can be processed. Also check the program's status in your [payout settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts): the payout goes through only when it's **Ready**. All the reasons are listed in [How to receive payouts](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts#why-is-my-payout-still-pending).

### Why is my commission still pending?

Your commission is pending because there is a [holding period](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/commissions-payouts#what-does-holding-period-mean) configured by the program (usually 30 days). After the holding period, the commission will be confirmed and eligible for payout.

### I referred a user but haven't received a commission, what should I do?

If you are certain that you followed the instructions to receive a commission, please wait 48 business hours for the commission to appear. If it is still missing, please reach out to your program and they will investigate.

### My payout failed — what should I do?

A payout is marked Failed when RocketWork or the bank couldn't transfer the money. Most often it's about the payout details:

- self-employed and sole proprietors — check your payout details in the RocketWork X app and the program's status in your [payout settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts): it must be **Ready**;
- legal entities — check the account details in the **Tax details** block of your payout settings.

Once the details are fixed, contact [RevRoute support](https://revroute.ru/contact/support) — the payout will be reconciled with the provider.

### Do you support payouts via PayPal, Payoneer, or crypto?

No. RevRoute pays partners exclusively in RUB: self-employed partners and sole proprietors through RocketWork, legal entities to a settlement account at a Russian bank. International payout methods are not supported.

## Payouts

### What payout destinations does RevRoute support?

It depends on the tax status you set in your [payout settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts):

- **self-employed** — through RocketWork, wherever you chose in the RocketWork X app: a card, SBP or an account;
- **sole proprietors** — through RocketWork, to the sole proprietor's settlement account;
- **legal entities** — to the company's settlement account through the RevRoute nominal account at T-Bank.

Payouts are sent in RUB. Individuals without a tax status can't be paid. Learn more in [How to receive payouts](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts).

### My payout was completed in RevRoute but the money hasn't arrived. What should I do?

1. Check your bank's notifications for the incoming credit.
2. Open your card or account statement for the payout date shown in RevRoute.
3. If the transfer isn't there, contact [RevRoute support](https://revroute.ru/contact/support) — we'll ask RocketWork or the bank for the payout status.

### Is tax withheld from my payout?

No. RevRoute doesn't withhold or remit taxes on the partner's behalf: payouts go only to self-employed partners, sole proprietors and legal entities, who pay their own taxes. For self-employed partners, RocketWork issues the NPD receipt for each payout.

The amount you'll receive is shown on the [Payouts](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts) page. If a platform fee was deducted from your earnings, the payout details show it separately.

### Can I switch payout methods at any time?

Yes:

- **self-employed and sole proprietors** — change the card or account in the RocketWork X app. The next payout will go to the new details;
- **legal entities** — update the account details in the **Tax details** block of your [payout settings](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts) and save.

If your tax status changes (for example, you've gone from self-employed to sole proprietor), choose the new status in the same place and fill in the fields. The program's status will be **Ready** again once RocketWork or the bank confirms the new details.
