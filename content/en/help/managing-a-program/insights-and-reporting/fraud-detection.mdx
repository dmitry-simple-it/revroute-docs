# Fraud detection

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Advanced plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

## Using fraud detection

The **Fraud protection** section of the program menu (under **Insights**) helps you catch and act on suspicious partner activity: RevRoute flags events, and you review the details and make the call.

While there are no flagged events, the section says "No events pending review".

![Fraud protection page with no events pending review](/images/ru/program/fraud/fraud-empty.jpg)

Once events appear, the table has these columns:

- **Event** — the fraud event type;
- **Partner** — the partner associated with the event;
- **Last detected** — the date and time of the most recent occurrence.

Each row is a single event type for a given partner and can contain multiple occurrences. If a partner is flagged for different events, they get several rows. You can filter the table by reason and partner.

## Fraud event types

| Event                                  | Description                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cross-program ban                      | The partner has been banned from one or more other programs, indicating a potentially high-risk history. Details such as links, evidence, or notes aren't shared between programs. |
| Duplicate payout method                | The partner uses a payout method already linked to another partner account, which may indicate duplicate accounts or fraud.             |
| Matching customer email                | The partner's email matches the customer's email — a possible self-referral.                                                            |
| Suspicious customer email domain       | The customer's email uses a disposable or temporary domain — a possible fraud attempt.                                                  |
| Banned referral source (configurable)  | The conversion, event, or click came from a banned referral domain.                                                                     |
| Paid traffic (configurable)            | The conversion, event, or click came from paid advertising traffic.                                                                     |
| Fraud report                           | Another program reported this partner for suspected fraud — e.g. when rejecting their application.                                     |

## Reviewing fraud events

<Warning>
  We recommend reviewing flagged events carefully and reaching out to the
  partner if needed before making a final decision.
</Warning>

Click any row in the table to open the **Event review** sheet. It has three blocks:

1. The partner associated with the event — from here you can **Message** them or open their **Partner profile**.
2. The event details: type, description, and a row for each occurrence of this type for the partner.
3. The partner's pending commissions that occurred after the flagged event.

## Resolving fraud events

Take your time and investigate before deciding. For example:

1. Compare partner and customer profiles to see whether they are duplicates or self-referrals.
2. Check your sales data for fraud signals (e.g. anti-fraud reports from your payment provider).
3. Ask the partner about their referral activity via our [built-in messaging center](/help/managing-a-program/engaging-partners/messaging-partners).

Once you're confident in your findings, choose one of the actions below.

### Resolve fraud event

If the event is benign, click **Resolve event** to mark it as safe and keep the partner in your program. When confirming, you can add a note on why the event was resolved and what you checked.

### Ban partner

If the fraud is confirmed, click **Ban partner** to mark the event as fraudulent and ban the partner from your program. Their other events pending review are resolved automatically.

You'll also need to pick a ban reason:

| Reason                              | When to use it                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Terms of Service violation          | The partner violated your program rules or the RevRoute terms of use.                                 |
| Inappropriate or offensive content  | The partner associated your brand or program link with harmful, explicit, or inappropriate content.   |
| Artificial traffic                  | The partner inflated traffic with bots, fake clicks, or other methods.                                |
| Fraudulent activity                 | The partner generated fake clicks, leads, or other tracked actions to earn rewards.                   |
| Spam or misleading content          | The partner used deceptive messaging, false claims, impersonation, or aggressive promotion.           |
| Brand or trademark violation        | The partner misused your brand name or trademarks or caused confusion.                                |

## Resolved fraud events

To view resolved events, open the **⋮** menu in the top right corner of the page and select **View resolved**. The **Resolved events** list shows when each event was resolved and the note left. Click any row to open the details.

## Fraud settings

Some detection rules can be configured: click **Fraud settings** in the top right corner of the page.

![Fraud settings sheet: Paid traffic and Referral source rules](/images/ru/program/fraud/fraud-settings.jpg)

### Paid traffic

This rule flags traffic from paid ads. If your program doesn't allow partners to run ads, turn it on and pick the ad networks to flag: Google, Facebook, X, Bing, LinkedIn, Reddit, and TikTok each have their own toggle.

#### Google campaign IDs whitelist

Add your internal Google Campaign IDs here to prevent your own Google Ads traffic from being flagged as fraudulent and associated with a partner.

<Tip>
  **Why this matters for Dynamic Search Ads (DSA):** If you run DSA campaigns, Google crawls your site and automatically selects landing pages to serve as ads. Sometimes Google indexes pages that already contain affiliate tracking parameters (e.g., `?via=affiliate`) from affiliate links being shared around the web.

When these pages are served as ads, the affiliate parameter carries through – making it appear that an affiliate is running unauthorized paid ads when it's actually your own campaign. Whitelisting your Google Campaign IDs ensures this internal traffic is correctly attributed to you and not flagged as partner fraud.
</Tip>

### Referral source

This rule flags traffic from specific domains. Turn it on and add the domains you want to monitor. Use the `*` wildcard to match any part of the address — handy for monitoring a specific page rather than all traffic from a domain.

### Disabling fraud settings

When you disable an active rule, RevRoute offers to mark the pending events for that rule as resolved.
