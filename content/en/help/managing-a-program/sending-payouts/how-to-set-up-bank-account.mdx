# Topping up the partner payout balance

<Tip>
  Partner payouts are available on the [Business plan and above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

RevRoute sends partner payouts from balances that you top up in advance with a transfer from your
company account: the payouts you [confirm](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts#how-to-pay-your-partners)
are paid from these balances. The card you use to pay for your RevRoute subscription is not involved
in payouts.

## Where payouts are paid from [#where-payouts-are-paid-from]

The balance depends on the partner's tax status:

| Partner | Where the money comes from | Where to see the balance |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-employed, sole proprietor** | Your company's RocketWork account balance | The **RocketWork account** card on the **Payouts** page |
| **Legal entity** | Your balance on the RevRoute nominal account at T-Bank | The **Nominal account — legal entities** card on the **Payouts** page |

Individuals without a tax status can't be paid through RevRoute.

To see which channels your program has, open **Payouts** → **Payout settings** → the **Payout
method** block: connected channels are marked **Connected**.

## If no payout channel is connected [#if-no-payout-channel-is-connected]

In that case the **Payout method** block says **No payout methods**, and the payout confirmation
window says **Payouts through RevRoute aren't connected**. To connect:

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Fill in the **Company details** block.
3. To pay legal entity partners, tick **I accept the terms of the agency agreement (Document No. 3)**.
4. Click **Save**.

If you've accepted Document No. 3, the nominal account is connected automatically after saving: the
form shows the bank's check of your company, then **Nominal account connected**.

The RocketWork account for paying self-employed partners and sole proprietors is opened by the RevRoute
team using your company details — you don't need to register with RocketWork or the bank yourself. To
speed things up, contact RevRoute support after saving your details. Once the account is open, the
**Payout method** block shows **RocketWork** marked **Connected**.

If you pay partners yourself outside RevRoute, you don't need a balance — choose the **Manual**
payout method ([learn more](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts#paying-partners-yourself)).

## How to top up the balance [#how-to-top-up-the-balance]

1. Open **Payouts** in the program menu.
2. On the **RocketWork account** or **Nominal account — legal entities** card, click **Top-up
   details**.
3. Click **Copy details** and transfer the money from your company account. For the nominal account,
   use the payment purpose exactly as shown — the payer's TIN links the transfer to your account
   automatically.
4. Wait for the balance on the card to update. The nominal account balance refreshes every 10
   minutes.

Step-by-step guides with screenshots: [Payout via RocketWork](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/rocketwork-payout#check-the-rocketwork-balance)
and [Legal entity payout](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/nominal-legal-entity-payout#check-the-nominal-account-balance).

## How much you need [#how-much-you-need]

The **Confirm payouts** window shows the totals for each invoice:

- **RocketWork** — the **RocketWork funding budget** line. Also allow for the RocketWork commission:
  it is debited from the same balance according to your plan.
- **Nominal account** — the **Legal entities receive** and **Fee (legal entities)** lines added
  together: payouts to legal entities plus the 5% RevRoute fee.

If the invoice includes partners of both types, you need money on both balances.

## If the balance is too low [#if-the-balance-is-too-low]

**When confirming.** While the balance is too low, the confirm button in the **Confirm payouts**
window is disabled, and a **Top up** link appears next to the balance.

1. Top up the balance that is short (see above).
2. Wait for it to update on the card.
3. Click **Confirm payouts** again.

If only one balance is short, you can exclude that channel's partners from the invoice with the
**Exclude** button in their rows and confirm the rest.

**After confirming.** Before paying a RocketWork task, RevRoute checks the RocketWork account balance
against the partner amount and the task commission once more. If the balance is short, the payout
stays **Processing** and is sent automatically once you top up the account.
