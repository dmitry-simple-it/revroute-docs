# amoCRM: connection

To let RevRoute pick up leads, payments and cancellations from amoCRM and calculate partner commissions automatically, you need three things:

1. A **long-lived token** of an external integration — RevRoute uses it to read your account's deals.
2. A **click ID** in the deal — it tells RevRoute which partner the customer came from.
3. **Deal stages** — which stages mean the deal is paid, refunded or canceled.

The integration is available on the Business plan and above. An amoCRM administrator connects it; it takes a few minutes.

## Token

### 1. amoMarket

In the amoCRM left menu, open **amoМаркет** (amoMarket).

### 2. Create integration

In the top right corner, click **«⋯»** and choose **«Создать интеграцию»** (Create integration).

![amoMarket: 1 — amoMarket in the menu, 2 — Create integration in the «⋯» menu](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-token-1-market.png)

### 3. External integration

In the **«Создание интеграции»** window, click **«+ Создать»** next to **«Внешняя интеграция»** (External integration). A private integration won't work: only an external one has a long-lived token.

![Create integration window: 3 — Create for the external integration](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-token-2-external.png)

### 4. Fill in the form and save

- **«Ссылка для перенаправления»** (Redirect URL) — `https://revroute.ru`. The field is required; RevRoute doesn't use it.
- **«Предоставить доступ: Все»** (Grant access: All) — tick the checkbox.
- **«Название интеграции»** (Integration name) — for example, `RevRoute`.

Click **«Сохранить»** (Save). amoCRM may ask you to confirm with a code from an email.

![External integration form: 4 — redirect URL, access «All», name and the Save button](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-token-3-form.png)

### 5. Generate a long-lived token

Open the new integration, go to the **«Ключи и доступы»** (Keys and access) tab, and in the **«Долгосрочный токен»** (Long-lived token) block click **«Сгенерировать токен»** (Generate token). Pick an expiry date — up to five years — and copy the token with the copy button next to the field.

![Keys and access tab: 5 — Generate token in the Long-lived token block](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-token-4-keys.png)

> The token is long — over a thousand characters. Copy it with the button rather than by selecting text: a selection easily misses part of it, and RevRoute won't accept a truncated token.

### 6. Integrations → amoCRM

In RevRoute, open **Settings → Integrations** and click the **amoCRM** card.

![Settings → Integrations: 6 — the amoCRM card](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-connect-1-integrations.png)

### 7. Account subdomain

Enter the part of your amoCRM address before `.amocrm.ru`: for `example.amocrm.ru` it's `example`.

### 8. Long-lived token

Paste the whole token from step 5.

### 9. Connect

Click **Connect**. RevRoute checks the token, creates the click ID field in deals and subscribes to deal changes.

![amoCRM connection form: 7 — subdomain, 8 — token, 9 — Connect](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-connect-2-form.png)

Once connected, the **Connection** block shows that the integration works, when the token expires and whether instant updates are on.

![Connection block: «Works» status and instant updates](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-connect-3-status.png)

> The token gives access to your amoCRM account — don't publish it or send it to open chats. If it leaked, delete the integration in amoCRM (**«Редактировать» → «Удалить интеграцию»**, Edit → Delete integration) — the old token stops working — create a new one following steps 1–5 and click **Replace token** in RevRoute. Stage roles and history are kept. A token that is about to expire is replaced the same way: generate a new one and click **Replace token**.

## Click ID in the deal

When connected, RevRoute creates a **«RevRoute: метка перехода»** (RevRoute: click ID) field in deals. This is the only change RevRoute makes to your amoCRM data.

![The «RevRoute: click ID» field in an amoCRM deal card](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-field-in-deal.png)

When a visitor follows a partner link, a click ID is stored in the browser on your site — the `rr_id` cookie (set by the [RevRoute script on your site](/en/docs/sdks/client-side/introduction)). For the customer to be credited to the partner, put this value into the **«RevRoute: метка перехода»** field together with the request:

- **A site form that sends requests to amoCRM.** Add a hidden field with the `rr_id` cookie value to the form and map it to the «RevRoute: метка перехода» deal field — the same way you pass UTM tags.
- **Your site creates deals via the API.** Pass the click ID by the field code `RR_CLICK_ID` when creating the deal (`POST /api/v4/leads`):

```json
[
  {
    "name": "Website request",
    "price": 15000,
    "custom_fields_values": [
      {
        "field_code": "RR_CLICK_ID",
        "values": [{ "value": "<rr_id cookie value>" }]
      }
    ]
  }
]
```

The click ID can also be added to a deal later: RevRoute notices the deal change and credits the customer to the partner. RevRoute takes the customer's name and email from the deal's main contact.

A deal from **«Неразобранное»** (Incoming leads) reaches RevRoute once a manager accepts it. Rejected requests won't show up for the partner.

## Deal stages

Open **Settings → Integrations → amoCRM**, the **Deal stages** block: it lists all pipelines of your account. Pick a role for each stage:

- **Paid** — a deal at this stage counts as a sale, and the partner earns a commission.
- **Refund** and **Cancel** — the deal's commission is canceled. The only difference is how the event is named in reports.
- Stages without a role are ignored.

For the standard amoCRM stages RevRoute suggests roles itself: **«Успешно реализовано»** (Closed – won) — Paid, **«Закрыто и не реализовано»** (Closed – lost) — Cancel. Accept the suggestions or pick your own and click **Save**.

![Deal stages block: 10 — Paid for «Closed – won», 11 — Cancel for «Closed – lost», 12 — Save](/images/ru/integrations/amocrm/amocrm-stages-roles.png)

Leads are loaded right after connecting; sales only come from stages with the Paid role.

**Payment isn't the last stage of the pipeline?** If a deal moves on after payment ("Documents", "Onboarding", "Closed – won"), choose **Paid: this and all following** for the payment stage — the role goes on it and every following stage of the pipeline except the lost ones. Then a deal that passed the payment stage quickly or skipped it still becomes a sale. There are no duplicates: one deal gives one sale, however many stages it passes.

After you save the roles, RevRoute re-reads the deals of the last 30 days with the new roles. Deal changes usually reach RevRoute within a minute, and RevRoute also checks deals itself every 5 minutes. The **Sync now** button runs the check immediately.

**Good to know:**

- The sale amount is the deal budget at the moment it reaches a Paid stage. If the budget changes after payment, the sale keeps the original amount.
- A deal moved back from Cancel to Paid doesn't create a new sale; the canceled commission stays canceled.
- If the token stops working (it was deleted in amoCRM, expired, or the amoCRM plan isn't paid), an error appears in the settings and we get a notification. Leads and sales from that period aren't lost: after the token is replaced, RevRoute continues where it stopped.
- If the commission of a canceled deal has already been paid out, RevRoute doesn't cancel it automatically — the team handles such cases manually.
