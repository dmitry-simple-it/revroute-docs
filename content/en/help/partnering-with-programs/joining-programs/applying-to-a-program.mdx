# How to join a partner program and set up payouts

The path from a program link to the first payout takes four steps: an application, a partner
account, approval by the program and your tax status. Without the last one, earnings accrue but are
not paid: only self-employed individuals, sole proprietors and legal entities can receive payouts.

## Apply to the program [#apply-to-the-program]

### 1. Apply on the program page [#1-apply-on-the-program-page]

Open the program link (`partners.revroute.ru/<program>`) and click **Apply**. The page lists the
reward terms, payout threshold and holding period; the program's offer is linked from the same page.

![Program page: 1 — the Apply button](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-1-program.png)

### 2. Name [#2-name]

The name the program will see you under: sole proprietors and companies may include the legal form.

### 3. Email [#3-email]

The verification code and program notifications go here. It also becomes your login.

### 4. Country [#4-country]

Start typing the name and pick the country from the list.

### 5. Status [#5-status]

Choose how you will receive payouts: **self-employed**, **sole proprietor** or **organization**.
Individuals without one of these statuses are not admitted.

![Application form: 2 — name, 3 — email, 4 — country, 5 — status](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-2-form.png)

### 6. Program terms [#6-program-terms]

Answer the program's questions (optional) and tick **I accept the program terms** — the link next to
it opens the offer.

### 7. Continue [#7-continue]

Click **Continue**. The application is saved; what's left is the account.

![Bottom of the form: 6 — accepting the terms, 7 — the Continue button](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-3-form-submit.png)

## Create a partner account [#create-a-partner-account]

### 8. Create account [#8-create-account]

On step 2 click **Create a RevRoute Partners account**. If you already have one, click **Sign in**
and the application attaches to it.

![Step 2 of 2: 8 — the Create a RevRoute Partners account button](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-4-create-account.png)

### 9. Password and code [#9-password-and-code]

The email is prefilled from the application. Set a password (at least 8 characters with a digit, an
uppercase and a lowercase letter), click **Create account** and enter the six-digit code from the
email. The code is valid for 10 minutes; if it didn't arrive, click **Resend** and check spam.

![Email verification: 9 — the six-digit code field](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-5-code.png)

Next the dashboard offers to add your website and social accounts — you can skip this and return to
it later in your profile.

## Wait for approval [#wait-for-approval]

### 10. Application submitted [#10-application-submitted]

In **Programs**, the program card shows **Application submitted** with the date. The program reviews
it; once approved, the card shows your partner link and the program sections appear in the left menu.
While the application is pending you can already set your tax status.

![Programs: 10 — the card with the Application submitted status](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-6-pending.png)

## Set your tax status [#set-your-tax-status]

### 11. Set status [#11-set-status]

Open **Payouts** in the left menu. Until the status is set, a warning **Set your tax status** stays
at the top of the page — click **Set status**. The same form opens from **Payout settings** at the top
right.

![Payouts page: 11 — the Set status button](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-7-tax-status.png)

### 12. Status [#12-status]

In **Tax details** choose **Self-employed**, **Sole proprietor** or **Legal entity**. Self-employed
and sole proprietors are paid through RocketWork, legal entities to a settlement account.

### 13. Tax ID, name and phone [#13-inn-name-and-phone]

For self-employed and sole proprietors: INN (12 digits), last name, first name, patronymic (if any)
and the phone number you are registered with in RocketWork. Legal entities fill in a different form:
company name, INN (10 digits), OGRN, address and bank details — see
[How to receive payouts](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts#fill-in-your-tax-details).

### 14. Save [#14-save]

Click **Save** in this block. The **Profile incomplete** badge changes to **Profile complete** and the
warning on the payouts page disappears.

![Payout settings: 12 — status, 13 — INN, name and phone, 14 — the Save button](/images/ru/partner/apply/apply-8-tax-form.png)

## What happens next [#whats-next]

For self-employed and sole proprietors RevRoute registers you as a contractor in RocketWork with every
program that pays through it. In the **RocketWork payouts** block the program gets a status: first
**Checking**, then **Ready** or a hint about what's missing (permissions in "Moy Nalog", bank details
in the RocketWork X app, a mismatch between INN and phone). What to do for each status is in the
[status table](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts#self-employed-and-sole-proprietors-rocketwork).

The program starts the payout once commissions pass the holding period and reach the minimum amount;
self-employed and sole proprietors get an email with a contract to sign before the first payout. You
don't need to withdraw anything manually.

> For legal entities the block is called **Settlement account payouts** and the status comes from the
> bank's registry check — see
> [Legal entities: payouts to a settlement account](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts#legal-entities-payouts-to-a-settlement-account).
