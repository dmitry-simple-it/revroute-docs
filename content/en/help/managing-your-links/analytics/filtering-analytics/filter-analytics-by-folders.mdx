# How to filter analytics by folders

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Pro plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

On RevRoute, you can filter analytics by [folders](/help/managing-your-links/links/link-folders). This allows you to create powerful campaign-specific reports for your links.

For example, you're using folders to group your affiliate links and SMS marketing links, and want to see how each of those campaigns are performing.

You can filter the analytics by each of those folders and get a real-time dashboard of their clicks and [conversion analytics](/docs/concepts/attribution).

---

Before you can filter analytics by folders, you need to [create a folder](/help/managing-your-links/links/link-folders#creating-a-folder) and [add links to it](/help/managing-your-links/links/link-folders#adding-links-to-a-folder).

Then, as your links start accruing clicks, you will be able to filter your analytics by folders.

<Steps>

    <Step title="Open the filter menu">
        In the [Analytics tab](https://app.revroute.ru/analytics) of your RevRoute workspace (the **Analytics** item under the **Statistics** section of the sidebar), click on the **Filter** button to open the filter menu:

        ![The Filter menu opened on the analytics page: Folder, Tag, Domain, Link and other filters](/images/ru/links/analytics-filter-dropdown.jpg)

        <Tip>
          You can also use the `F` keyboard shortcut to quickly open the filter menu.
        </Tip>
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select the 'Folder' filter and pick a folder">
        Select the **Folder** option in the menu and check the folder you want to filter by. A filter chip appears above the chart, the metrics recalculate, and a **Share** button shows up in the toolbar — you can use it to [share the folder's dashboard](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/share-analytics):

        ![The Folder submenu in the analytics filter: the "Партнёрские ссылки" folder is selected and the metrics are recalculated](/images/ru/links/analytics-filter-folder.jpg)
    </Step>

</Steps>

## Opening folder analytics directly

Alternatively, you can also open the analytics for a folder directly: open the `⋮` menu next to the folder in your links dashboard and select the **Analytics** option:

![The folder row menu with the Analytics and Share analytics options](/images/ru/links/folder-row-menu.jpg)

Voila! You have successfully filtered your analytics by folder. You can also [export your analytics data](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/how-to-export-analytics) to a CSV file for further analysis.
