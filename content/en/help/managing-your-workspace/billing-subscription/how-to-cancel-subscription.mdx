# How to cancel my RevRoute subscription?

First of all, if you are planning to cancel your subscription, we're really sorry to see you go! We would appreciate any feedback on how we could improve our platform – feel free to [send us an email](mailto:support@revroute.ru?subject=Feedback%20about%20RevRoute)!

## How can I cancel my RevRoute subscription?

Subscriptions are set up separately for each workspace. To cancel yours:

1. In your workspace, open **Settings** → **Billing**.
2. Click the **⋮** button next to **View invoices** and select **Cancel subscription**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

![Billing page ⋮ menu: Manage subscription and Cancel subscription](/images/ru/workspace/billing-menu.jpg)

After you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the paid period — a banner with the end date appears on the **Billing** page. After that, the workspace moves to the Free plan. If you change your mind, select **Resume subscription** in the same menu.

We really hope to see you back someday!

## How can I change my RevRoute plan?

To switch to another plan, including a lower one:

1. In your workspace, open **Settings** → **Billing**.
2. Click **Manage plan** — on the free plan, the button is called **Upgrade**.
3. Choose a plan and a billing period — monthly or yearly.

Enterprise plans don't have a plan change button: Enterprise terms are negotiated individually, so [contact us](https://revroute.ru/contact/support) to change them.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please [contact our support team](https://revroute.ru/contact/support).
