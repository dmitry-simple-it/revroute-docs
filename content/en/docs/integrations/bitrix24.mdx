# Bitrix24: connection

To let RevRoute pick up leads, payments and refunds from Bitrix24 and calculate partner commissions automatically, you need three things:

1. An **inbound webhook** — a link RevRoute uses to read your portal's CRM.
2. A **click ID** in the lead and the deal — it tells RevRoute which partner the customer came from.
3. **Deal stages** — the stages at which a deal counts as paid, refunded or canceled.

The integration is available on the Business plan and above.

## Webhook

A portal administrator creates the webhook in a couple of minutes. Create it from an administrator account: the webhook acts with that user's permissions.

### Shortcut

Open this link with your portal address — it opens the new-webhook form directly; continue from [step 3](#3-crm-access):

```
https://<your-portal>.bitrix24.ru/devops/edit/in-hook/
```

### Via the menu

#### 1. Developer resources → "Other"

In the portal's left menu open **"Разработчикам"** (Developer resources) and click the **"Другое"** (Other) tile.

![Bitrix24 developer resources, the "Other" tile highlighted](/images/ru/integrations/bitrix24/bitrix24-webhook-1-devops.jpg)

#### 2. "Inbound webhook"

![The "Other" section, the "Inbound webhook" tile highlighted](/images/ru/integrations/bitrix24/bitrix24-webhook-2-other.png)

#### 3. CRM access

In **"Настройка прав"** (Access permissions) click **"+ выбрать"** (+ select) and pick **CRM (crm)**. No other permissions are needed.

![Inbound webhook form: 3 — CRM access, 4 — Create button, 5 — webhook link](/images/ru/integrations/bitrix24/bitrix24-webhook-3-form.png)

#### 4. Save

Click **"Создать"** (Create) at the bottom of the form (for an existing webhook the button is **"Сохранить"**, Save).

> **Important.** Without this step the permissions are not saved: the link opens, but it has no access to the CRM.

#### 5. Paste the link into RevRoute

Copy the **"Вебхук для вызова rest api"** field — a link like `https://<your-portal>.bitrix24.ru/rest/1/<code>/`.

In RevRoute open **Settings → Integrations → Bitrix24**, paste the full link and click **Connect**. RevRoute checks CRM access and creates the click ID field itself (see below). If the field could not be created, check the webhook's CRM permission and click **Check again**.

> The link grants access to the portal's CRM — do not publish it or send it to open chats. If it leaked, click **"Перегенерировать"** (Regenerate) in the same Bitrix24 form: the old link stops working, and in RevRoute you can swap it with **Replace URL** (stage roles and sync history are kept).

## Click ID in the lead and the deal

On connection RevRoute creates a **"RevRoute: метка перехода"** field (in an English-language portal it is named **"RevRoute click ID"**) in leads and deals. This is the only change RevRoute makes to your CRM.

When a visitor follows a partner link, the browser stores a click ID on your site — the `rr_id` cookie (set by the [RevRoute script on your site](/en/docs/quickstart/client)). For the customer to be credited to the partner, put this value into the **"RevRoute: метка перехода"** field together with the request:

- **Bitrix24 CRM form on your site.** Add a hidden field mapped to "RevRoute: метка перехода" and fill it with the `rr_id` cookie value (the same way you pass UTM tags).
- **Custom form.** Pass `rr_id` in the field when creating the lead via REST:

```json
{
  "fields": {
    "TITLE": "Website request",
    "NAME": "John",
    "UF_CRM_RR_CLICK_ID": "<rr_id cookie value>"
  }
}
```

- **Your site creates deals, skipping leads.** Write the click ID straight into the deal, in the same `UF_CRM_RR_CLICK_ID` field: RevRoute checks both the lead and the deal.

When a lead is converted into a deal, the value moves with it. A lead whose click ID was added by hand after creation is not picked up by RevRoute — set the click ID on the deal instead.

## Deal stages

Open **Settings → Integrations → Bitrix24**, the **Deal stages** block: it lists all pipelines of your portal. Choose a role for each stage:

- **Paid** — a deal at this stage counts as a sale, the partner earns a commission.
- **Refunded** and **Canceled** — the deal's commission is canceled. The only difference is how the event is named in reports.
- Stages without a role are ignored.

Until at least one stage is marked Paid, deals are not imported (leads always are).

**Is payment not the last stage of your pipeline?** Often a deal moves on after "Invoice paid": "Documents to ship", "In training", "Successfully completed". If the Paid role sits only on "Invoice paid", a deal that passed it between two checks, or skipped it, will not become a sale. So for the payment stage choose **"Paid: this and all following"** — the role is set on it and on all following stages of the pipeline except unsuccessful ones. There are no duplicates: one deal gives one sale, however many stages it passes.

After you save the roles, RevRoute re-reads deals from the last 30 days with the new roles. After that it checks every 5 minutes; **Sync now** runs a check immediately.

**Good to know:**

- RevRoute only **reads** the CRM. It does not change deals or stages; on connection it creates just the two click ID fields (in leads and in deals).
- The webhook acts as the administrator account that created it. If that employee is removed or blocked, the webhook stops working: create a new one and replace the link in RevRoute.
- If the webhook stops working, an error appears in the settings and our team is notified. Nothing is lost: after the fix RevRoute resumes from where it stopped.
- If the commission of a canceled deal has already been paid out, RevRoute does not cancel it automatically — the team handles such cases by hand.
