# Inviting partners to your program

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Business plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

There are a few ways you can invite partners to join your affiliate/referral program on RevRoute Partners:

1. [Inviting via the dashboard](#inviting-via-the-dashboard)
2. [Enrolling a partner automatically](#enrolling-a-partner-automatically)
3. [Via a branded application form](#via-a-branded-application-form)

## Inviting via the dashboard

The easiest way to invite a partner to join your program is by sending them an invitation email.

Open **All partners** in the program menu and click **Invite partner** (or press `P`).

![Invite partner button on the partners page](/images/ru/program/partners-invite.jpg)

The **Invite partner** sheet opens. Enter the partner's email and, optionally, their name, short link (the address of their referral link), and the [group](/help/managing-a-program/getting-started/partner-groups) they're being added to. Below is a preview of the email the partner will receive; it's sent from `notifications@revroute.ru`.

![Invite partner sheet with an email preview](/images/ru/program/invites/invite-partner-sheet.jpg)

### Bulk Invitations

Invite multiple partners at once by separating their email addresses with commas or pasting a list into the **Email** field. Each partner receives an individual invitation email.

### Updating invitation email

You're also able to edit the email that is sent to the invited partner. To do this, click **Edit** in the **Email preview** section.

![Invitation email editor: subject, title, and content](/images/ru/program/invites/invite-partner-email-edit.jpg)

You can change the email **Subject**, **Title**, and **Content**. Style the content with the buttons under the editor: bold, italic, and link. Changes apply only to this invite — they don't replace the default content.

Click **Save** to update the email content, or **Cancel** to revert back to the default content. Once your invite is ready to send, click **Send invite**. Your partner will receive an email in their inbox to join your program.

### Viewing past invites

To view your previous invites, on the **All partners** page click **Filter**, then **Status**, then **Invited**.

The **⋯** menu on an invited partner's row lets you resend the invite, delete the invite, or change the group. Deleting an invite can't be undone.

![Invited partner menu: resend and delete the invite](/images/ru/program/invites/partners-invited-menu.jpg)

## Enrolling a partner automatically

Instead of sending an invitation email and waiting for them to accept it, you can also automatically enroll known partners in your program.

A few use cases for this approach:

- Programmatically adding your apps users to your [in-app referral program](/docs/partners/embedded-referrals)
- Migrating your partners over from another platform like [Rewardful](/help/managing-a-program/migration-guides/migrating-from-rewardful)

If you're integrating with RevRoute Partners programatically, you can use our POST /partners API endpoint to enroll a partner into your program via our API.

<Warning>
  Before auto-enrolling a partner to your program, make sure to get their
  consent – either explicitly or via the acceptance of your Terms of Service.
  Enrolling partners that are unfamiliar with your program is against our [terms
  of service](https://revroute.ru/legal/terms).
</Warning>
