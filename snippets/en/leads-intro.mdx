In [conversion tracking](/docs/concepts/attribution), a `lead` event is an
action that signals interest in your product:

- signing up for an account;
- booking a demo;
- joining a mailing list.

The lead event establishes the customer's identity and the link they came from:
every later action (a purchase, a plan upgrade) is attributed to that same link.
