# Messaging your partners

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Advanced plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

On RevRoute, you can use the messaging center to communicate directly with the partners in your program. Partners get notified about new messages via email and reply to you from their partner dashboard.

## Messages location

In your program menu, open **Messages** to see all your conversations with partners and compose new messages.

## Messages overview

The messaging center has three panels:

![Messaging center: conversation list, conversation with a partner, and the Profile panel](/images/ru/program/messages/messages-center.jpg)

| Panel        | Content                                         |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Messages     | All your conversations in one place.            |
| Conversation | The full thread with your selected partner.     |
| Profile      | Key info and performance stats for the partner. |

### Conversation list

The left panel lists every conversation with your partners: the partner's name and profile image, plus the date and an excerpt of the latest message. Select a conversation to pick up where you left off, or click the **Pencil** button in the top right of the panel to compose a new message — it opens a search for partners in your program.

### Conversation panel

The center panel shows the full thread with your partner. Your workspace teammates can message partners too if their [role](/help/managing-your-workspace/workspace-members/workspace-roles) allows it, with their profile images shown beside each message.

Type your message in the field at the bottom of the panel and click **Send**. The field also has emoji, bold, italic, and link buttons.

### Message status

Each message sent has a check mark:

- **Single check mark** – delivered
- **Double check marks** – delivered and read (either in the notification email or in the partner dashboard)

### Profile panel

The right panel shows the partner's status in your program, their email, group, and performance stats: clicks, leads, conversions, revenue, commissions, and net revenue. Click **Change group** to move the partner to another group, or **View profile** to open their full profile. You can hide the panel with the button in the top-right corner of the conversation.

## Message notifications

RevRoute notifies you about new partner messages via email. You can turn the **New message from partner** notification on or off in **Settings** → **Notifications**, under **Partner program**.

## Disabling messaging

If you prefer to handle support outside RevRoute, you can disable partner messaging.

Go to your program's **Resources**, turn off the **Messages** switch in the **Help and support** section, and click **Save**.

![Help and support section on the Resources page: support email and the messaging switch](/images/ru/program/resources/help-and-support.jpg)

When messaging is disabled, partners see a notice in their dashboard that your program uses external support, with a button to email the support address specified in that section. Your **Messages** page shows that messaging is disabled, with a button to go to **Resources** and turn it back on.
