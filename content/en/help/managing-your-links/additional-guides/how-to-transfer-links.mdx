# How to transfer links between workspaces?

Transferring links between workspaces is a common use case for many RevRoute users, especially when they want to reorganize their links or consolidate them into a single workspace.

This guide will show you how to transfer your RevRoute short links from one workspace to another.

<Warning>
  Transferring a link will also **fully reset its stats** and is irreversible
  – please proceed with caution.
</Warning>

## Step 1: Open the link transfer modal

For a given link, click on the `⋮` button and select **Transfer**.

![Link row actions menu with the transfer option](/images/ru/links/link-row-menu.jpg)

You can also use the `T` keyboard shortcut to open the transfer modal.

## Step 2: Select the destination workspace

In the **Transfer link** modal, use the **Select workspace** selector to pick the workspace you want to transfer the link to:

![Transfer link modal](/images/ru/links/link-transfer-modal.jpg)

To verify, type `confirm transfer link` in the confirmation field and click **Transfer link** to complete the transfer.

That's it! Your link should now be successfully transferred to the new workspace.

## Caveats

- Transferring a link will also **fully reset its stats** and is irreversible – please proceed with caution.
- You can only transfer links on the default RevRoute domain (`link.revroute.ru`) between workspaces. If you want to transfer a custom domain link, you'll need to [transfer the domain](/help/managing-your-links/custom-domains/how-to-transfer-domains) itself to the new workspace.
- Link tags are not transferred when you transfer a link between workspaces. You'll need to re-apply the tags to the link in the new workspace.
- You can only transfer links to workspaces that you have access to. If you don't have access to the destination workspace, you won't be able to transfer the link.
