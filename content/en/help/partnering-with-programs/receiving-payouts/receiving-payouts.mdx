# How to receive payouts from programs

On RevRoute, payouts go to **self-employed individuals, sole proprietors and legal entities** — with a
receipt or closing documents issued to the company whose program you promote. An individual without
one of these statuses can't be paid.

To get your money:

1. Fill in your tax details in the payout settings.
2. Get the payout status for each program to "Ready".
3. When a program sends a payout, sign the contract via the link (self-employed and sole proprietors).

You don't need to withdraw anything manually: the program starts the payout once your commissions
clear the holding period and reach the minimum payout amount.

## Where to set up payouts [#where-to-set-up-payouts]

Open [partners.revroute.ru/payouts](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts) and click
**Payout settings** in the top right. The panel has three blocks:

- **Tax details** — your status and details;
- **Payouts via RocketWork** (or **Payouts to a settlement account** for legal entities) — readiness
  for each program you're in;
- **Invoice details (optional)** — what to print on payout invoices, see
  [Customizing invoice details](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/partner-payout-invoice).

## 1. Fill in your tax details [#fill-in-your-tax-details]

In the **Tax details** block, choose your **Status** and fill in the fields:

| Status | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Self-employed** | Tax ID (INN, 12 digits), last name, first name, middle name (if any), phone |
| **Sole proprietor** | Tax ID (INN, 12 digits), last name, first name, middle name (if any), phone |
| **Legal entity** | Company name, Tax ID (INN, 10 digits), OGRN, KPP (if any), legal address, registration date, bank details: BIC, bank name, settlement and correspondent accounts |

Click **Save** in this block. A **Profile complete** badge appears next to the heading.

<Warning>
  Self-employed and sole proprietors: the phone must be the one your RocketWork account is registered
  to, and the INN must match the one in the "My Tax" (Мой налог) app. If they don't match,
  RocketWork rejects the details (status "Check your details").
</Warning>

## 2. Self-employed and sole proprietors: RocketWork status [#self-employed-and-sole-proprietors-rocketwork]

Programs pay self-employed partners and sole proprietors through
[RocketWork](https://help.rocketwork.ru/): each payout comes with a one-off contract, and the
self-employed tax receipt is generated automatically. Once you save your tax details, RevRoute
registers you as a contractor with each program.

The **Payouts via RocketWork** block shows a status for each program. A payout goes through only
when it's **Ready**. What to do otherwise:

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Tax details needed** | Fill in your tax details above. |
| **Checking** | Nothing — the check usually takes a few minutes. |
| **Permissions needed** | Click **Request permissions in My Tax** and approve the request in the "My Tax" app. |
| **Not self-employed** | The tax service doesn't see you as self-employed: register in "My Tax" and come back. |
| **No bank details** | Add a card or account for payouts in the RocketWork X app. Self-employed can use SBP, a card or an account; sole proprietors — a settlement account only. |
| **Check your details** | Make sure your INN and phone match "My Tax" and RocketWork. |
| **Tax unpaid** | Pay the outstanding tax in "My Tax" — payouts are blocked until then. |
| **Payouts blocked** | Contact RocketWork support — they know the reason. |
| **Not connected** | Nothing: this program doesn't pay via RocketWork yet. |
| **Setup incomplete** | Nothing: the program is still setting up payouts. |

Your RocketWork account, "My Tax" permissions and bank details are shared across all programs: set
them up once and you'll be **Ready** everywhere a program pays via RocketWork.

## 3. Legal entities: payouts to a settlement account [#legal-entities-payouts-to-a-settlement-account]

Legal entities are paid to the company's settlement account through RevRoute's nominal account at
T-Bank. Instead of the RocketWork block, the settings show **Payouts to a settlement account** with a
status for each program. The bank checks the company against the state register: if the status is
**Bank rejected**, double-check the INN and OGRN in your tax details.

## 4. Signing the contract and getting paid [#signing-the-contract-and-getting-paid]

Self-employed and sole proprietors: once the program approves a payout, a **Waiting for your
signature** section appears in the payout settings with the program and amount. Click **Sign** — it
opens RocketWork, where you sign with a code from an SMS. The money is sent only after you sign.

Legal entities don't need to sign anything: the money goes to the settlement account once the
payout is approved.

You can track payout status on the [Payouts](https://partners.revroute.ru/payouts) page.

## FAQs [#faqs]

### Why is my payout still pending? [#why-is-my-payout-still-pending]

There can be several reasons, all of them visible:

- the commission is still in the **holding period** set by the program (e.g. 30 days in case of a refund);
- you haven't reached the program's **minimum payout amount**;
- your status for this program in **Payout settings** isn't **Ready**;
- the program hasn't approved the payout yet.

Ask the program about its holding period and minimum payout amount.

### Can I receive payouts as an individual without a tax status? [#can-i-receive-payouts-as-an-individual-without-a-tax-status]

No. Only self-employed individuals, sole proprietors and legal entities can be paid. You can become
self-employed in a few minutes in the "My Tax" app.

### What currency are payouts in? [#what-currency-are-payouts-in]

Rubles only, to Russian cards and accounts.

### Do I need to withdraw my earnings manually? [#do-i-need-to-withdraw-my-earnings-manually]

No. The program starts the payout. All you need is a **Ready** status and to sign the contract when it
arrives.
