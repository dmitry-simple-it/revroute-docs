# How to set up a redirect for your custom domain on RevRoute

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Pro plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

When you add a custom domain to your workspace, its root address (the domain without a slug, e.g. `go.acme-demo.ru`) does not redirect anywhere by default — such a domain shows a "No redirect configured" label in the domains list.

Note that the root domain redirect is configured not in the "Update domain" modal, but by editing the domain's root link.

To redirect your root domain to a URL of your choice, follow these steps:

1. If you haven't already, upgrade to the [Pro plan](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
2. Open the [**Settings → Domains**](https://app.revroute.ru/settings/domains) page.
3. Click on the `⋮` button next to the domain you want to redirect, which will open a dropdown menu.

![Domain menu with "Link settings" and "Domain settings" sections](/images/ru/links/domain-row-menu.jpg)

4. In the "Link settings" section, click **Edit link** — this opens the [RevRoute link builder](/help/managing-your-links/additional-guides/link-builder) for the domain's root link.
5. Enter the URL you want to redirect your domain to in the **Destination URL** field (marked with a PRO badge).

![Root domain link builder with the Destination URL field](/images/ru/links/root-domain-redirect.jpg)

6. Click **Save changes**. Your root domain will now redirect to the URL you just specified, and the "No redirect configured" label next to the domain will disappear.
