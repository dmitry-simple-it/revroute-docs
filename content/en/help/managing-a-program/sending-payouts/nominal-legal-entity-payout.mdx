# How to pay a legal entity partner through the nominal account

Programs pay company partners to their settlement account through RevRoute's nominal account at
T-Bank. No contracts or receipts per payout: the money comes from your balance on the nominal account
and the bank transfers it to the company's account. From you — a commission, enough balance and the
payout confirmation.

By now the partner has set the Legal entity status and bank details, and the bank has checked the
company — the partner shows **Ready** (see the partner's side in
[Legal entity payouts](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/payouts-legal-entity)). You can
check readiness yourself too: the partner card has a **Payout readiness** line, and the partner list
shows a tooltip on the partner's icon.

## Create a commission [#create-a-commission]

Commissions usually appear on their own — from sales through the partner's links. A one-off payout
(bonus, reimbursement) can be created manually.

### 1. Create commission [#1-create-commission]

Open **Commissions** in the program menu and click **Create commission** (or press `C`).

![Commissions: 1 — the Create commission button](/images/ru/program/rocketwork-payout/rw-payout-1-commissions.png)

### 2. Partner [#2-partner]

Pick the company partner from the list — start typing its name.

### 3. One-off type [#3-one-off]

**One-off** — a bonus or reimbursement not tied to a sale. It is available for payout right away: the
holding period doesn't apply.

### 4. Amount [#4-amount]

Enter the amount in rubles. The description is optional — the partner sees it.

### 5. Create commission [#5-save]

Click **Create commission**. The commission appears in the list, and **Payouts** shows a pending payout
to the partner.

![Create commission: 2 — partner, 3 — One-off type, 4 — amount, 5 — the Create commission button](/images/ru/program/rocketwork-payout/rw-payout-2-create.png)

## Check the nominal account balance [#check-the-nominal-account-balance]

### 6. Balance and top-up details [#6-balance-and-top-up]

Open **Payouts**. At the top is the **Nominal account — legal entities** card with your balance. If it
doesn't cover the pending payouts, click **Top-up details** and transfer money from your company
account. Incoming transfers are matched automatically; the balance refreshes every 10 minutes.

### 7. Confirm payouts [#7-confirm-payouts]

The **Pending payouts** card shows the amount ready for payout and, below it, the program's minimum
payout. Amounts below the minimum accumulate until the next period; change the threshold with the
**Change** link. Click **Confirm payouts**.

![Payouts: 6 — nominal account balance and top-up details, 7 — the Confirm payouts button](/images/ru/program/nominal-payout/nom-payout-3-balance.png)

## Confirm the payout [#confirm-the-payout]

### 8. Check the totals [#8-check-the-totals]

The **Confirm payouts** window shows where the money comes from and how much the company receives:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | Commissions earned by partners for the period |
| **Legal entities receive** | What arrives to the companies' settlement accounts |
| **Fee (legal entities)** | RevRoute's 5 % fee on payouts to legal entities |
| **Total** | The amount of the whole invoice |

Your nominal account balance is debited by the **Legal entities receive** and **Fee (legal
entities)** lines added together. If the invoice also has self-employed partners or sole
proprietors, RocketWork lines appear next to these.

### 9. Press and hold [#9-press-and-hold]

Press and hold the button at the bottom of the window until it fills up. While the balance is short,
the button is disabled — top up the nominal account (step 6) and come back.

![Confirm payouts: 8 — legal entity totals, 9 — the confirm button](/images/ru/program/nominal-payout/nom-payout-4-confirm.png)

## Transfer and statuses [#transfer-and-statuses]

### 10. RevRoute approves the payout [#10-revroute-approves-the-payout]

After confirmation you see **Payout confirmed**. The payout gets the **Processing** status: RevRoute
reviews and approves it, then the bank creates a deal on the nominal account and transfers the money.
The partner receives a payout email.

### 11. Statuses on the Payouts page [#11-payout-statuses]

After approval the payout becomes **Sent**, and once the money reaches the company's account —
**Completed**, with the date in the **Paid** column. The nominal account balance decreases by the total
from step 8.

![Payouts: 11 — status Completed](/images/ru/program/nominal-payout/nom-payout-5-status.png)

General rules — minimum amount, holding period, payout statuses — are in
[Sending partner payouts](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts). Self-employed
partners and sole proprietors are paid through RocketWork — [Payout via RocketWork](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/rocketwork-payout).
