# How to export link analytics data from RevRoute

Analytics can be powerful in understanding how your audience is interacting with your content and to make data-driven decisions.

For RevRoute, it's always been a top priority to provide [world-class analytics](/help/managing-your-links/analytics/analytics-overview) to everyone.

To get more out of your analytics, you can export your analytics data to a CSV file. This can be useful if you want to analyze the data in a business intelligence (BI) tool, share it with your team, or import it into a spreadsheet.

## Exporting link analytics data

To export data from your link analytics, follow these steps:

<Steps>

<Step title="Go to the analytics page">

Go to the [RevRoute dashboard](https://app.revroute.ru), log-in, and choose the project you want to export data from.

Open the **Analytics** item under the **Statistics** section of the left sidebar.

</Step>

<Step title="Download the data">

Click on the `⋮` button in the top-right corner of the analytics page to open up the more options dropdown menu.

Click on the **Download CSV** option.

![The ⋮ menu on the analytics page with the Download CSV option](/images/ru/links/analytics-export.jpg)

After a few seconds, an archive will be downloaded to your computer. It contains a CSV file for each analytics breakdown you know from the dashboard.

</Step>

</Steps>
