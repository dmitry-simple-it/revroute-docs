# How to export links from RevRoute

In your RevRoute workspace, you can export your links to a CSV file. This is useful if you want to analyze the data locally or share them with an external stakeholder.

To export your links, follow these steps:

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the Export links modal">

    In your RevRoute workspace, navigate to the **Links** page.

    Then, click on the `⋮` button next to the search bar to open the more options dropdown menu. Select **Export links** → **Export to CSV**.

    ![The ⋮ menu on the links page with the import and export options](/images/ru/links/links-export-menu.jpg)

</Step>

<Step title="Select the date range">

    In the **Export links** modal, choose the **Date Range** for the links you want to export. By default, **All Time** is selected — RevRoute will export all links.

    ![The Export links modal with the date range, columns, and the Apply current filters toggle](/images/ru/links/export-links-modal.jpg)

</Step>

<Step title="Select the columns to export">

    RevRoute will export the following columns by default:
    - Short link
    - Destination URL
    - Clicks
    - Created at

    You can change the set of columns under the **Columns** section — **Leads**, **Conversions**, **Revenue**, **Link ID**, **Updated at**, **Tags**, and **Archived** are also available.

</Step>

<Step title="Apply current filters" optional>

    Optionally, enable the **Apply current filters** toggle to export only the links that match the current dashboard filters. If the toggle is off, all links will be exported.

</Step>

<Step title="Download the data">

    Once you have selected the date range and columns to export, click on **Export links**.

    After a few seconds, the CSV file will be downloaded to your computer.

</Step>

</Steps>
