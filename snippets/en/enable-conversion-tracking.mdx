First, enable conversion tracking for your links — without it a click is never
linked to the lead and sale events that follow.

<Tip>
  You can skip this step for partner program links: RevRoute enables conversion
  tracking on them automatically.
</Tip>

**For the whole workspace.** Open
[**Settings → Analytics**](https://app.revroute.ru/settings/analytics) and turn on
the **Conversion tracking** switch in the page header — it controls the
workspace-level setting. Every link created from that point on has conversion
tracking enabled.

**For a single link.** Open the
[link builder](/help/managing-your-links/additional-guides/link-builder) and turn
on the **Conversion tracking** switch — inside the builder it is also bound to
the `C` keyboard shortcut.

**Via the API.** Pass `trackConversion: true` when creating or updating a link
(it defaults to `false`):

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.revroute.ru/links" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer dub_xxxxxx" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "url": "https://example.com",
    "trackConversion": true
  }'
```
