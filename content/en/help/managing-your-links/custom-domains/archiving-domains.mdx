# Archiving domains on RevRoute

You can archive your custom domains on RevRoute to better manage your domains.

When you archive a domain:

- The domain will not show up in the [link builder](/help/managing-your-links/additional-guides/link-builder)
- The domain will be hidden from the **Active** list on the domains page
- Its links will continue to work and will still show up on the links dashboard

To archive a domain, follow the steps below:

1. Go to [**Settings → Domains**](https://app.revroute.ru/settings/domains).
2. Click the `⋮` button next to the domain you want to archive and, under the **Domain settings** section, select **Archive**.

![Domain menu: the domain settings section with the archive option](/images/ru/links/domain-row-menu.jpg)

3. Confirm the archiving in the dialog that appears.

The domain will now be archived and will no longer show up in the active domain list. You can find it under the **Archived** tab on the domains page and unarchive it from there.
