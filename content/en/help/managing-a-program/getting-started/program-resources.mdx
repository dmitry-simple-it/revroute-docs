# Configuring brand and support resources for your partners

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Business plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

Set your partners up for success by providing support, clear documentation, program terms, and brand assets in their dashboard, so they can get started faster, avoid mistakes, and represent your brand consistently.

## Where to find program resources

In your program menu, open **Resources** under **Engagement**. The page has two sections: **Help and support** and **Brand assets**.

## Help and support

To give your partners the best experience, fill in every field in this section and click **Save**.

![Help and support section: support email, messaging, help center, and terms of service](/images/ru/program/resources/help-and-support.jpg)

### Support email

The address partners use to contact you with questions about your program. **This field is required.**

Partner replies to your [program emails](/help/managing-a-program/engaging-partners/email-campaigns) go to this address. In the partner dashboard, it's available in the **Program support** block via the **Email support** button.

### Messages

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Advanced plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

The **Enable partner messaging** switch turns on the [messaging center](/help/managing-a-program/engaging-partners/messaging-partners): you can message partners directly in RevRoute, and they can message you. When messaging is off, partners see a button to email your support address instead.

![Messaging center: conversation list, conversation with a partner, and the Profile panel](/images/ru/program/messages/messages-center.jpg)

### Help center

A link to documentation or other self-serve resources that help your partners succeed. In the partner dashboard, it appears in the **Program support** block as **Help center**. The address in the field is just an example — enter your own.

### Terms of service

A link to your partner program's terms of service (usually separate from your product's general terms). In the partner dashboard, it appears in the **Program support** block.

This address is also used in the required **Agree to program terms** checkbox of your [program application form](/help/managing-a-program/recruiting-partners/program-application-form). Until it's set, the form editor shows an **Add link** button next to the checkbox that leads to the **Resources** page.

![Agree to program terms checkbox in the application form editor with the Add link button](/images/ru/program/branding/application-consent.jpg)

## Brand assets

Add logos, useful links, brand colors, and other files — partners can download and use them from their dashboard.

![Brand assets section: logos, links, colors, and additional files](/images/ru/program/resources/resources-brand-assets.jpg)

Each asset you add has a menu for downloading or opening, copying, editing, and deleting it.

### Brand logos

Upload your official logos so partners always have the latest versions. Click **Add logo**, select or drop a file (SVG, JPG, PNG, or WEBP, up to 5 MB), and enter the **File name**.

![Add logo dialog: logo file and name](/images/ru/program/resources/resources-add-logo.jpg)

### Links

Add links to materials that live on your website — for example, brand guidelines or a banners page. Click **Add link**, then enter the **URL** and the **Display name** partners will see.

![Add link dialog: the URL and the name partners will see](/images/ru/program/resources/resources-add-link.jpg)

### Colors

Share your brand colors. Click **Add color**, pick a color from the palette or enter its **Hex** code, and enter the **Color name**.

![Add color dialog: palette, Hex code, and color name](/images/ru/program/resources/resources-add-color.jpg)

### Additional files

Add any other materials for your partners — a document or a ZIP archive of up to 10 MB. For example:

- Brand guidelines
- Product presentation
- Banner pack

Click **Add file**, select or drop a file, and enter the **File name**.

![Add file dialog: file and name](/images/ru/program/resources/resources-add-file.jpg)
