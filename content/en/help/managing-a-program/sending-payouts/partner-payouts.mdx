# Sending partner payouts on RevRoute

<Tip>
  This feature is only available on [Business plans and
  above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

RevRoute Partners pays partners in one click: you top up the payout balance and confirm the invoice,
and RevRoute sends each payout through RocketWork or the nominal account at T-Bank, prepares
documents for your accounting and shows the status of every payout.

If you're running a program with hundreds of partners, you don't need to transfer money to each of
them separately or track failures by hand.

## Supported payout methods

The payout channel depends on the tax status the partner sets in their
[payout settings](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts):

| Partner | How RevRoute pays | Where the money comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Self-employed** | Through RocketWork: a one-off contract for each payout, which the partner signs with an SMS code; the NPD receipt is generated automatically. The money goes where the partner chose in the RocketWork X app: a card, SBP or an account. | Your company's RocketWork account balance |
| **Sole proprietor** | Through RocketWork: a one-off contract for each payout, signed with an SMS code; the money goes to the sole proprietor's settlement account. | Your company's RocketWork account balance |
| **Legal entity** | From the RevRoute nominal account at T-Bank to the company's settlement account. No contracts or receipts per payout. | Your balance on the nominal account |

Individuals without a tax status can't be paid through RevRoute.

To see which channels your program has, open **Payout settings** → the **Payout method** block:
**RocketWork** and **T-Bank nominal account** are marked **Connected**. If there are none, fill in
**Company details** under **Settings → Billing** — see
[Topping up the payout balance](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/how-to-set-up-bank-account#if-no-payout-channel-is-connected).

Step by step for each channel:

- [Payout via RocketWork](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/rocketwork-payout) — self-employed partners and sole proprietors;
- [Legal entity payout](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/nominal-legal-entity-payout) — through the nominal account.

<Note>
  Payouts are sent in **RUB**.
</Note>

## Paying partners yourself

If you pay a partner yourself, outside RevRoute, choose the **Manual** payout method:

- for the whole program — **Payout settings** → **Default payout channel** → **Manual**;
- for a single partner — on the partner's page.

These payouts carry a **Manual** badge in the table. Once you've paid the partner, select the payouts
in the table and click **Mark as paid**. The partner doesn't need a tax status or a RocketWork or
nominal account connection for this, and RevRoute charges no fee on these payouts.

## Tax handling

Partners pay their own taxes:

- **Self-employed (NPD regime)** — RocketWork issues the NPD receipt for each payout; the partner
  doesn't need to issue it in the Moy Nalog ("My Tax") app. Tax: 4% on payments from individuals, 6%
  on payments from businesses.
- **Sole proprietor (IP)** — declares the income under their own tax regime.
- **Legal entity** — declares the income under its own tax regime.

<Warning>
  RevRoute does not withhold or remit taxes on the partner's behalf. You can see whether a partner is
  ready for payouts on their page — the **Payout readiness** row.
</Warning>

## Payouts dashboard

To view your payouts, go to your **Partner Program** tab and click on the **Payouts** page.

At the top of the page, you'll see your balance cards (**RocketWork account**, **Nominal account — legal entities**) with a **Top-up details** button, and a summary of your pending and completed payouts:

**Pending payouts**

This shows the total amount of commissions that are eligible for payment.

Hovering over the total will show you:

- **Eligible payouts** – due commissions that have reached the minimum amount, for partners who have [connected payouts](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts).
- **Ineligible payouts** – the rest: the partner hasn't [connected payouts](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts) yet (no tax status, or RocketWork or the bank hasn't confirmed their details), or the amount is below the minimum.

Once you have at least one pending payout on RevRoute, you can click the **Confirm payouts** button to [review and confirm them](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts#how-to-pay-your-partners).

**Total paid**

This shows the total amount of all commissions you've paid out to your partners.

Hovering over the total will show you:

- **Completed payouts** — payouts that RocketWork or the bank has carried through to the end
- **Processing payouts** — payouts that are still in progress

Click **View invoices** to access past payout invoices.

In the payout table below, you can track and manage your payouts, confirm pending ones, and review past activity.

![Payouts page: summary and payouts table](/images/ru/program/program-payouts.jpg)

| Column  | Description                                                                                                                 |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Period  | The payout range, starting from the date of the partner's first commission to the end of the month of their last commission |
| Partner | The partner receiving the payout                                                                                            |
| Status  | The current payout [status](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts#payout-statuses)                       |
| Paid    | The date the payout was confirmed, and by whom                                                                              |
| Amount  | The total amount paid to the partner                                                                                        |

## Payout statuses

A payout can have one of the following statuses:

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | The payout hasn't been confirmed yet: it's waiting for your confirmation, for the partner to [connect payouts](/help/partnering-with-programs/receiving-payouts/receiving-payouts), or for the amount to reach the [minimum](#minimum-payout-amount). |
| Processing | The payout was [confirmed](#how-to-pay-your-partners) and RevRoute is setting it up with the provider: in RocketWork, a one-off contract waiting for the partner's signature; on the nominal account, RevRoute's approval and a bank transaction. |
| Sent | RocketWork or the bank is transferring the money to the partner. |
| Completed | The money has reached the partner. |
| Failed | The provider didn't carry out the payout. Contact [RevRoute support](https://revroute.ru/contact/support) — the payout will be reconciled with the provider. |
| Canceled | The payout was canceled — for example, when a partner is banned from the program. |

<Tip>
  Statuses are updated automatically from RocketWork and bank data.
  There's nothing for you to do.
</Tip>

## Payout holding period

On RevRoute, you can also set a "payout holding period" to specify how long your commissions will be held in "pending" status before becoming eligible for payout.

This is useful for safeguarding against potential refunds, chargebacks, or fraudulent transactions.

<Info>
  If a sale tracked via your billing system is refunded, RevRoute will mark the [corresponding commission as
  refunded](/help/managing-a-program/rewards-and-commissions/partner-commissions#commission-statuses)
  — provided you've passed `invoiceId` when tracking the original sale.
</Info>

To set a payout holding period for your program, go to your [individual groups](/help/managing-a-program/getting-started/partner-groups) and click on the **Settings** tab. Here you'll find the payout holding period settings in the "Additional settings" group.

![Group additional settings: payout holding period](/images/ru/program/group-holding-period.jpg)

Your options include the following:

- 0 days (default)
- 7 days
- 14 days
- 30 days
- 60 days
- 90 days

When you update the payout holding period, you can apply the new setting to every group at once. Just select the box labelled `Apply to all groups` before confirming. The change will take effect right away.

![Confirm holding period change](/images/ru/program/holding-period-confirm.jpg)

## Minimum payout amount

You can also set an optional "minimum payout amount" (e.g. 1 000 ₽). When this is set, partners with earnings below the minimum payout amount won't be eligible for payout until they reach the specific threshold set.

Click the button in the top right to show the **Payout settings**. Unlike the payout holding period, this setting is program-wide.

![Payout settings button](/images/ru/program/payout-settings-button.jpg)

## How to pay your partners

When one or more of your partners have earned more than the minimum payout amount, click **Confirm payouts** from the [payouts dashboard](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts#payouts-dashboard) to open the payout details.

- **Method:** where the money comes from — **RocketWork balance** and/or **Nominal account balance**, with the current balance. If it's too low, a **Top up** link appears next to it — see [Topping up the payout balance](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/how-to-set-up-bank-account).
- **Cutoff Period:** If set, only commissions earned on or before this date will be paid. Useful for reconciliation payouts for a specific fiscal period (e.g. last month, last quarter) without including newer commissions.
- **Partners**, **Amount**, **RevRoute fee**, the amounts per channel and **Total** — the invoice summary. What each line means is explained in [Payout via RocketWork](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/rocketwork-payout#8-check-the-totals) and [Legal entity payout](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/nominal-legal-entity-payout#8-check-the-totals).

If everything looks right, press and hold the button at the bottom of the window until the payout is confirmed. While the balance is too low, the button is disabled.

After confirmation, RevRoute sets up each payout with the provider separately: for self-employed partners and sole proprietors, a one-off contract in RocketWork that the partner signs with an SMS code; for legal entities, a nominal account transaction after RevRoute's approval. You can follow each payout by its [status](#payout-statuses).

### Excluding payouts

In some cases, you may want to exclude a partner's payout from the current batch. To do this, hover over the partner's row in the payouts table and click **Exclude**. This removes the partner from the current payout batch.

If you need to add the partner back, hover over their row and click **Include**. This adds them back to the current payout batch.

## Payout fees and timing

- **RevRoute fee** — 5% of payouts made through RevRoute. The amount for each invoice is shown in the **Confirm payouts** window. Payouts you make [manually](#paying-partners-yourself) carry no fee.
- **RocketWork commission** is debited from your RocketWork account balance according to your plan.
- **Nominal account** — the amount is sent to the bank in whole rubles.

Self-employed partners and sole proprietors get the money only after they sign the contract in RocketWork. Legal entities — after RevRoute approves the payout.

## Topping up the payout balance

To pay partners through RevRoute, top up your RocketWork account balance (for self-employed partners and sole proprietors) and/or the nominal account (for legal entities) in advance with a transfer from your company account. The details are on the **Payouts** page — the **Top-up details** button on the balance card.

RevRoute checks the balance when you confirm payouts and won't let you confirm the invoice if it's too low. Learn more in [Topping up the payout balance](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/how-to-set-up-bank-account).
