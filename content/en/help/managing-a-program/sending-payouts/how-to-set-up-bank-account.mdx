<Tip>
  Partner payouts are available on the [Business plan and above](https://revroute.ru/pricing).
</Tip>

With the [RevRoute partner program](/help/managing-a-program/dub-partners), you [confirm payouts](/help/managing-a-program/sending-payouts/partner-payouts) to your partners in a single click. You don't need to set up bank transfers, accounts, or API credentials — payouts are charged to the **same card you use to pay for your RevRoute subscription**.

## How it works

1. You click **Confirm payouts** on the `Payouts` page.
2. RevRoute creates an invoice covering the total payout amount plus the platform fee.
3. The amount is charged to the card attached to your workspace.
4. After the charge succeeds, each partner receives their share on the card linked to their account.

The status of every payout is visible on the partner's page and under [Billing → Invoices](https://app.revroute.ru/settings/billing/invoices?type=partnerPayout).

## Which card is used

A workspace has **one card on file** — the same card used for recurring subscription charges. It's used for:

- monthly/yearly subscription renewals;
- one-time charges for plan upgrades;
- partner-payout invoices on every confirmation.

You can see the card under [Billing → Payment method](https://app.revroute.ru/settings/billing).

## How to replace the card

<Steps>

<Step title="Open billing settings">
Go to [Billing → Payment method](https://app.revroute.ru/settings/billing).
</Step>

<Step title="Click “Replace card”">
A secure widget will open where you enter a new card. Once submitted, RevRoute will stop charging the old card.
</Step>

<Step title="Verification charge">
To confirm the new card works, RevRoute makes a 1 ₽ micro-charge and refunds it immediately. After that the new card becomes the primary one.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
  Cards are added and replaced only through a secure widget — RevRoute never sees or stores raw card data. Only the last 4 digits and the brand (Visa / MasterCard / MIR) are visible.
</Note>

## If a charge fails

If the card has insufficient funds, is expired, or has been blocked, partner payouts **will not start** and the invoice will be marked **Failed**.

What to do:

1. Replace the card following the steps above.
2. On the invoice page, click **Retry payment** — RevRoute will attempt the charge again.

If a partner hasn't linked a card yet, they won't receive their share. They need to link a card from their personal cabinet (`/programs/<slug>/payouts/card`); once they do, the payout will be retried automatically on the next confirmation.
