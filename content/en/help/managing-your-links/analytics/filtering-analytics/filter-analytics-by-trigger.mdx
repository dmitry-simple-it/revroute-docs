# How to filter between QR code scans and link clicks

On RevRoute, you can filter between QR code scans and link clicks in your RevRoute analytics. This lets you understand how people are finding your links.

Here's how you can do that:

<Steps>

    <Step title="Go to your Analytics dashboard">
        Open the **Analytics** item under the **Statistics** section of the sidebar in your RevRoute workspace.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Open the filter menu">
        Then, click on the **Filter** button to open the filter menu:

        ![The Filter menu opened on the analytics page: Folder, Tag, Domain, Link and other filters](/images/ru/links/analytics-filter-dropdown.jpg)
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select the filter by event type">
        In the list, select the filter by event type (link click or QR code scan) and check the option you need — the dashboard recalculates and shows only the selected event type. To find the filter quickly, start typing its name in the menu's search field.
    </Step>

    <Step title="Compare event types on the Triggers tab">
        The split between link clicks and QR code scans is also visible on the **Triggers** tab among the analytics breakdowns:

        ![The analytics breakdown tabs, including "Триггеры" (Triggers)](/images/ru/links/analytics-facets.jpg)
    </Step>

</Steps>

Voila! You have successfully filtered your analytics by either QR code scans or link clicks.
